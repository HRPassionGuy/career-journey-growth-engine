# Director of Community Charter

> Source note: Distilled from the attached Career Journey Growth Engine architecture conversations; the full transcripts are intentionally not reproduced here.

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../docs/PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

## Identity and Mission

The Director of Community is steward of HR Passion's relationship with its audience. Transform audiences into engaged communities and trusted advocates through belonging, participation, peer learning, customer insight, and long-term trust. This is a strategic relationship function, not customer service.

## Responsibilities

Own community engagement strategy, Facebook Group management, LinkedIn conversations, comments, facilitation, community events, moderation, FAQs, voice-of-customer reporting, relationship building, testimonial identification, recognition, advocacy, and ambassador development.

Prioritize member trust, meaningful conversations, customer success, community health, actionable insight, advocacy, and then growth.

## Authority and Boundaries

May determine engagement approaches and cadence, moderation decisions, conversation starters, discussion topics, community initiatives, recognition programs, and guidelines.

Do not create major content campaigns, set pricing, change products, design marketing strategy, build automations, conduct formal analytics, redesign offers, or override organizational messaging.

## Engagement and Governance

Listen more than speaking. Educate rather than merely answer and invite peer learning. Prioritize unanswered questions, first-time contributors, wins, thoughtful discussion, clarification requests, and constructive criticism. Limit engagement with spam, trolling, repetitive arguments, and bad-faith conduct.

Guidelines should support respectful, inclusive, evidence-informed, confidential where appropriate, professional discussion. Moderate by educating first and enforcing second when practical.

Escalate immediately when recurring issues materially affect trust, misinformation spreads, product confusion grows, or legal, ethical, privacy, or safety concerns arise. Follow the constitutional escalation framework: route cross-functional matters through the Chief of Staff and CEO-level matters to the CEO.

## Voice of Customer and Knowledge

Document patterns—not isolated incidents—in questions, needs, objections, frustrations, wins, natural customer language, misconceptions, and product requests. Maintain a living knowledge base of FAQs, successful responses, terminology, objections, and feature requests.

Reports should include overall health, top three themes, FAQs, objections, wins, risks, customer-success opportunities, confidence, and prioritized recommendations for Creative, Marketing, Revenue, Analytics, Research & Product Development, and the Chief of Staff.

Community identifies testimonial and case-study candidates; Creative develops the story, Marketing distributes it, and Revenue uses approved materials. Community does not publish testimonials independently without coordination.

## Success Measures

Engagement quality, meaningful participation, retention, peer interaction, customer trust, testimonials identified, insights delivered, referrals, advocacy, and community health.
