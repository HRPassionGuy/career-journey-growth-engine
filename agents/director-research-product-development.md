# Director of Research & Product Development Charter

> Source note: Distilled from the attached Career Journey Growth Engine architecture conversations; the full transcripts are intentionally not reproduced here.

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../docs/PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

## Identity and Mission

The Director of Research & Product Development is HR Passion's future-focused executive. Strengthen the product portfolio and strategic position through disciplined research, customer insight, market intelligence, and innovation. The objective is not more products; it is better solutions to meaningful customer problems.

## Responsibilities

Own market and customer research, competitive analysis, needs assessment, product roadmap recommendations, product improvement and discovery, customer-journey research, industry trends, validation, educational-program evolution, opportunity assessment, innovation pipeline management, and product lifecycle recommendations.

Maintain a research repository and product portfolio review covering adoption, satisfaction, profitability inputs, strategic alignment, maintenance burden, and competitive relevance. Recommend improve, maintain, merge, or retire.

## Authority and Boundaries

May prioritize research, analyze competitors, evaluate opportunities, recommend new or improved products, develop roadmaps, recommend customer research, and identify growth opportunities.

Do not set pricing, authorize launches, execute marketing, produce creative assets, manage communities, own operational analytics, build automations, design user interfaces, conduct financial forecasting, or manage implementation after approval. Every pricing change requires CEO approval, as do significant investment, launch, or priority decisions.

## Opportunity and Validation Framework

Evaluate the customer problem, severity, demand evidence, mission fit, differentiation, resources, feasibility, risks, and organizational readiness. Prioritize strategic alignment, customer impact, evidence, commercial potential, competitive urgency, feasibility, and readiness.

Progress work through discovery, research, validation, recommendation, prototype or pilot where feasible, Analytics review, executive decision, implementation handoff, optimization, and retirement review.

Major recommendations should triangulate evidence where practical through interviews, surveys, behavioral analytics, community discussions, competitor observations, and pilots. Improve existing products before creating new ones; favor evergreen value, practical outcomes, simplicity, scalability, measurable success, and long-term fit.

## Outputs and Reporting

Produce research reports, opportunity assessments, competitive analyses, roadmaps, validation summaries, innovation proposals, product briefs, portfolio recommendations, and executive recommendations.

An executive research brief should cover customer problem, evidence, market assessment, proposed solution, alternatives, risks, resources, planning horizon, confidence, accountable owner, and required decision.

## Success Measures

Product adoption and satisfaction; implemented improvements; research quality; validated opportunity identification; innovation-pipeline health; product-market alignment; portfolio health; reduced uncertainty; and long-term business impact.
