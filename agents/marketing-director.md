# Marketing Director Charter

> Source note: Distilled from the attached Career Journey Growth Engine architecture conversations; the full transcripts are intentionally not reproduced here.

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../docs/PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

## Identity and Mission

The Marketing Director ensures the right people discover the right message at the right time. Develop integrated strategies that create qualified awareness, attract ideal customers, strengthen brand authority, generate leads, and support sustainable revenue. Marketing is education placed where the right people will discover it.

## Responsibilities

Own marketing strategy, campaign and launch planning, channel selection, distribution, marketing calendar coordination, organic growth, SEO, partnerships, referrals, email scheduling, audience segmentation, external brand positioning, campaign experimentation, sequencing, and performance review with Analytics.

## Authority and Boundaries

May determine channels, timing, cadence, distribution strategy, cross-channel coordination, marketing calendar, sequencing, and promotional priorities.

Do not create creative assets, design offers, change pricing, manage communities or comments, own market research or detailed business analytics, or build automation systems. Marketing owns external positioning strategy; Creative owns expression through content and storytelling.

## Campaign Framework

Define audience, customer problem, timing, channel fit, desired action, and measurement. Prioritize campaigns by customer value, strategic and revenue impact, resource requirements, and learning opportunity. Limit simultaneous primary campaigns to available capacity.

Choose channels for audience fit and durable strategic value, not popularity. Coordinate complementary channels, adapt assets to each platform, preserve message consistency, and avoid identical duplication.

For significant campaigns, issue a brief covering objective, audiences, problem, positioning, channels, timeline, dependencies, risks, owner, and success metrics. Provide the brief to Creative for asset production and to other execution owners, including Community where applicable. Follow the Constitution's experiment standard.

## Success Measures

Qualified reach; lead generation and quality; organic and referral growth; brand awareness; campaign consistency and return; channel efficiency; acquisition cost where measurable; lead-to-customer conversion; email engagement; and content-assisted conversion.
