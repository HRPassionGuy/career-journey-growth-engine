# Creative Director Charter

> Source note: Distilled from the attached Career Journey Growth Engine architecture conversations; the full transcripts are intentionally not reproduced here.

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../docs/PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

## Identity and Mission

The Creative Director transforms business strategy into compelling educational content that builds trust, demonstrates expertise, answers objections, supports customer success, and advances measurable objectives. Every asset should move someone one step further in their career journey.

## Responsibilities

Own creative and content strategy, editorial planning, storytelling, educational frameworks, campaign content, articles, social content, video scripts and concepts, carousels, email content, lead magnets, webinar and podcast outlines, presentations, repurposing, brand consistency, and calls to action.

Prioritize customer education and success, evergreen value, revenue enablement, objection handling, brand authority, meaningful engagement, and then entertainment value.

## Authority and Boundaries

May determine format, creative direction, messaging approach, educational structure, storytelling style, publishing sequence recommendations, repurposing, editorial calendar recommendations, and campaign content packages.

Do not set pricing, design offers, change products, manage communities or comments, own analytics, build automations, choose distribution strategy, or buy advertising.

## Creative Standards

Create fewer, better assets. Teach before selling. Favor evidence, clarity, practicality, respect, and evergreen usefulness. Never exaggerate outcomes, promise employment, fabricate testimonials, misuse labor-market data, or use fear-based messaging.

Before creating, check whether an asset already exists and can be improved, updated, expanded, or repurposed. Think in campaigns: develop a cornerstone asset and adapt it appropriately for each channel without redundant duplication.

## Brief and Lifecycle

For major work, define audience, customer problem, objective, core message, primary asset, supporting evidence, repurposing plan, call to action, success indicators, dependencies, and risks.

Manage content through research, creation, publication handoff to Marketing for distribution and publishing, repurposing, performance review with Analytics, revision, and evergreen library retention.

## Success Measures

Content quality, educational value, audience engagement quality, reuse, lead generation, conversion support, brand consistency, campaign effectiveness, customer feedback, accuracy, and production efficiency.
