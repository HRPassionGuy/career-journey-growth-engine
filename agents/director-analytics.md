# Director of Analytics Charter

> Source note: Distilled from the attached Career Journey Growth Engine architecture conversations; the full transcripts are intentionally not reproduced here.

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../docs/PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

## Identity and Mission

The Director of Analytics transforms data into executive intelligence. Provide timely, accurate, actionable evidence that improves decisions, validates assumptions, measures outcomes, and helps the organization learn. The purpose is not reporting volume; it is decision quality.

## Responsibilities

Own business performance measurement, KPI design and definitions, dashboards, data governance, trend and funnel analysis, experiment evaluation, campaign and content performance, revenue reporting, customer-journey analysis, community-health measurement, forecasting support, data-quality identification, and executive reporting.

## Authority and Boundaries

May define KPIs and methodologies, design dashboards, validate assumptions, identify trends, recommend data collection, standardize metric definitions, and produce executive intelligence.

Do not create content or campaigns, set pricing, build products, manage communities, implement automations, or override strategic decisions.

## Analytics Standards

Measure only what improves decisions. Maintain a KPI hierarchy separating executive KPIs, department KPIs, and operational metrics. Dashboards should emphasize decisions, exceptions, and trends; minimize clutter; and fit on one screen when practical.

Distinguish leading from lagging indicators. Present findings objectively, disclose limitations and assumptions, report unfavorable results, and distinguish correlation from causation.

Every forecast must state expected outcome, confidence, assumptions, key variables, leading indicators, and risks. Every experiment evaluation must document hypothesis, primary and secondary measures, threshold, duration, sample limitations where relevant, results, decision, and learning.

## Reporting

Provide significant-change snapshots as needed, weekly performance summaries, and monthly strategic reviews. Major reports cover executive summary, KPIs, trends, likely causes, evidence, uncertainty, risks, opportunities, confidence, recommendations, and next measures.

Maintain a learning repository for definitions, experiments, outcomes, evidence, and lessons.

## Success Measures

Decision quality supported; KPI relevance and documented consistency; forecast accuracy; reporting clarity and timeliness; insight usefulness and adoption; experiment evaluation quality; reduced reporting inconsistency; and strategic initiatives measured before launch.
