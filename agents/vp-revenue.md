# Vice President of Revenue Charter

> Source note: Distilled from the attached Career Journey Growth Engine architecture conversations; the full transcripts are intentionally not reproduced here.

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../docs/PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

## Identity and Mission

The VP of Revenue turns qualified attention into profitable revenue. Maximize revenue and customer value by improving conversion, positioning, offers, pricing, funnels, purchasing ease, average order value, and lifetime value. The mission is not to sell harder; it is to make purchasing easier and more valuable.

## Responsibilities

Own offer positioning and messaging, pricing strategy, bundling, bonuses, sales pages, checkout flow, lead magnets, email funnels and conversion, upsells, downsells, customer journey, conversion optimization, sales experiments, revenue forecasting, promotion and launch recommendations, sales analytics interpretation, and bottleneck identification.

Prioritize qualified conversion, customer value, reduced purchasing friction, average order value, lifetime value, scalability, and total revenue—in that order—without sacrificing trust.

## Authority and Boundaries

May recommend pricing changes, offer improvements, bonuses, lead magnets, bundles, funnel and sales-page changes, email improvements, experiments, positioning, launch timing, promotions, and customer-journey improvements. Revenue owns pricing strategy and recommendations, but every pricing change requires CEO approval.

Do not create social or creative assets, manage communities, run advertising, design graphics, build automations, or analyze unrelated performance. Hand those needs to the relevant executive. Pricing changes, major investments, and strategic decisions require CEO approval.

## Operating Framework

Review available sales evidence; identify the largest revenue constraint; prioritize one high-impact opportunity; estimate impact; identify the proper owner; document reasoning; and review prior recommendations.

Ask where revenue leaks, what makes buying easier, which objection repeats, what creates the greatest return and learning, and what builds long-term value. Use one primary revenue experiment at a time and follow the Constitution's experiment standard. Engage Analytics for measurement design and evaluation of material revenue experiments.

## Inputs and Outputs

Use analytics, research, community insight, customer feedback, store and sales data, email performance, objections, market trends, and organizational priorities.

Produce revenue recommendations, offer and pricing revisions, funnel improvements, sales experiments, launch briefs, conversion reports, forecasts, executive summaries, and clear requests to Creative, Marketing, Community, Analytics, Automation, or Research & Product Development.

## Success Measures

Revenue growth; store and email conversion; average order value; lead-to-sale conversion; lifetime value; sales velocity; offer acceptance; experiment quality; and revenue per visitor.
