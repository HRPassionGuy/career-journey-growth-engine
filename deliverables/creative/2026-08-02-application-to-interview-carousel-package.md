# Asset 1 — Instagram Carousel

**Working title:** 100 Applications, Few Interviews? Audit These Four Things  
**Audience:** Qualified professionals applying consistently but not reaching enough first interviews  
**Objective:** Help the viewer diagnose message clarity before increasing application volume  
**Recommended account:** Existing authorized HR Passion Instagram business account; Marketing must confirm the exact handle before the final action

## Exact six-slide copy

### Slide 1 — Hook

**100+ applications.  
Few interviews.**

Before you send more, audit these four things.

**Visual direction:** Make `100+` and `Few interviews` the dominant contrast. Add a small `SWIPE →` cue.

**Alt text:** Cover slide asking job seekers with more than 100 applications and few interviews to audit four parts of their positioning before applying more.

### Slide 2 — Target role

**1. TARGET ROLE**

Can someone name the role you want within a few seconds?

If your résumé tries to fit five different careers, your strongest evidence gets harder to recognize.

**Visual direction:** Show one clear target in focus and several faded role labels around it.

**Alt text:** Step one asks whether the target role is immediately clear instead of trying to position for many unrelated roles.

### Slide 3 — Top-third proof

**2. TOP-THIRD PROOF**

Words like “strategic,” “passionate,” and “results-driven” are claims.

Give the reader context for the problems you solve.

**Visual direction:** Cross out three generic adjectives and replace them with `problem + context + proof`.

**Alt text:** Step two contrasts generic adjectives with concrete context and evidence in the top third of a résumé.

### Slide 4 — Specific outcomes

**3. SPECIFIC OUTCOMES**

“Managed onboarding” names a responsibility.

Explain what changed: fewer delays, clearer steps, better adoption, or another truthful result.

Use numbers only when they are accurate.

**Visual direction:** Use a before-and-after text treatment; emphasize `what changed?`.

**Alt text:** Step three shows the difference between naming a responsibility and explaining a specific truthful outcome.

### Slide 5 — Human voice

**4. HUMAN VOICE**

AI can help you compare or edit.

It should not make qualified people sound interchangeable.

Keep your judgment, examples, and language you can defend in an interview.

**Visual direction:** Contrast a generic repeated paragraph with one highlighted authentic proof statement.

**Alt text:** Step four encourages responsible AI assistance while preserving the applicant’s authentic judgment and defensible examples.

### Slide 6 — CTA

**DO NOT JUST APPLY MORE.  
MAKE YOUR EVIDENCE EASIER TO SEE.**

For a structured way to audit your positioning, résumé, interview preparation, and next move:

**Use the link in bio and select the YOU COACHING SERIES.**

**Visual direction:** Strong headline, simple four-item recap, and one visually distinct CTA button treatment. Do not display price unless Marketing verifies Revenue authorization at action time.

**Alt text:** Final slide encourages a clearer evidence-based application strategy and directs viewers to the existing YOU COACHING SERIES through the link in bio.

# Asset 2 — LinkedIn Carousel

**Working title:** A Qualified Résumé Can Still Hide the Reason to Interview You  
**Audience:** Experienced professionals, career changers, and HR practitioners refining their professional narrative  
**Objective:** Teach a professional evidence chain that connects target role to interview-ready proof  
**Recommended account:** Existing authorized Marcus Holmes LinkedIn profile; Marketing must confirm the exact profile URL and posting identity before the final action

## Exact six-slide copy

### Slide 1 — Hook

**A QUALIFIED RÉSUMÉ CAN STILL HIDE  
THE REASON TO INTERVIEW YOU.**

Use this evidence chain.

**Visual direction:** Professional editorial layout with `qualified` and `reason to interview` emphasized.

**Alt text:** Cover slide explaining that a qualified résumé can still obscure the evidence that supports an interview decision.

### Slide 2 — Role clarity

**START WITH ROLE CLARITY**

Name the role and the business problems you are prepared to solve.

Without a clear target, even strong experience can look disconnected.

**Visual direction:** A simple arrow from `target role` to `business problem`.

**Alt text:** First step connects a clear target role with the business problems the candidate can solve.

### Slide 3 — Proof near the top

**MOVE PROOF NEAR THE TOP**

Replace broad self-descriptions with evidence:

- scope
- context
- relevant expertise
- credible outcomes

**Visual direction:** Four stacked evidence blocks, with no icons that imply unverified credentials.

**Alt text:** Second step recommends placing scope, context, relevant expertise, and credible outcomes near the top of the résumé.

### Slide 4 — Responsibility to result

**TURN RESPONSIBILITY INTO EVIDENCE**

Do not stop at what you owned.

Explain the problem, your judgment, your action, and the truthful result.

**Visual direction:** Horizontal chain: `problem → judgment → action → result`.

**Alt text:** Third step turns a responsibility into evidence by connecting the problem, judgment, action, and truthful result.

### Slide 5 — Interview-ready test

**USE THE INTERVIEW-READY TEST**

Can you explain every major claim with a specific example?

If not, revise it before a recruiter asks.

Clarity on paper should survive a human conversation.

**Visual direction:** Show a résumé statement flowing into a speech bubble with a concise example.

**Alt text:** Fourth step asks whether every major résumé claim can be supported with a specific example during an interview.

### Slide 6 — CTA

**YOUR EXPERIENCE IS NOT THE PROBLEM TO SOLVE.  
CLARITY MAY BE.**

Build a clearer connection between your experience, résumé, interview stories, and next move.

**Explore the existing YOU COACHING SERIES through the authorized HR Passion product link.**

**Visual direction:** Recap the evidence chain and present one restrained CTA. Do not display a price or urgency claim without action-time Revenue confirmation.

**Alt text:** Final slide encourages clearer connections between experience, résumé evidence, interview stories, and the existing YOU COACHING SERIES.
