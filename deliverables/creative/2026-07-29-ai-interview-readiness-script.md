## Final asset

**Title:** Do not let an AI interview flatten your experience  
**Estimated delivery:** 80–95 seconds  
**On-screen hook:** `Facing an automated interview? Prepare proof stories—not robotic answers.`

### Final spoken copy

If your next interview is conducted by phone, text, or video automation, do not prepare by memorizing perfect-sounding answers.

Prepare three concise proof stories.

For each story, write down four things.

First: the problem. What was happening, and why did it matter?

Second: your judgment. What did you notice, decide, or prioritize that a job description would never reveal?

Third: your action. What did you personally do—not just what the team did?

Fourth: the truthful result. What changed, what did you learn, or what would you improve? Use numbers only when they are accurate.

Now practice each story in three lengths: one sentence for a text screen, about thirty seconds for a phone response, and about sixty seconds for video.

The goal is not to sound like a machine because a machine may be listening. The goal is to make your evidence clear in an unfamiliar format while keeping your voice, context, and judgment intact.

And remember: never share confidential employer, employee, or customer information just to make a story sound impressive.

Save this and build your three proof stories before the next screening request arrives.
