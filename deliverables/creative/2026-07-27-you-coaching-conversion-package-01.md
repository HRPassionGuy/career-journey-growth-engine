### Asset 1 — Résumé positioning diagnostic

**Canonical title:** Your résumé may be making the recruiter do the work  
**Hook:** `Qualified—but still not getting interviews? Run this 3-part résumé diagnostic.`  
**Teaching beats:** Target role; top-third proof; specific truthful outcomes  
**CTA:** `If you want a structured way to audit your positioning, résumé, interview preparation, and next-career move, go to the link in my bio and select the YOU COACHING SERIES.`

**Exact caption**

Qualified experience can still be difficult to recognize when your target role is unclear, the top of your résumé relies on adjectives, or your bullets stop at responsibilities.

Before you send more applications, audit three things:

1. Can someone name the role you are targeting?
2. Does the top third show evidence—not just claims?
3. Do your bullets explain specific, truthful outcomes?

The goal is not more keyword stuffing. It is clearer evidence of fit.

For a structured way to work through your positioning, résumé, interview preparation, and next move, use the link in my bio and select the YOU COACHING SERIES.

### Asset 2 — Interview judgment diagnostic

**Canonical title:** Your interview answer may explain the work but hide your judgment  
**Hook:** `If your interview answers sound like job descriptions, use this diagnostic.`  
**Teaching beats:** Problem; individual role; decision; truthful outcome; lesson  
**CTA:** `If you want a structured way to prepare your career story, interview examples, and next move, go to the link in my bio and select the YOU COACHING SERIES.`

**Exact caption**

An interview answer can describe your responsibilities and still hide the judgment that made your work valuable.

For each example, make five things clear:

1. What was the problem?
2. What was your role—not only the team’s role?
3. What decision did you make?
4. What specific, truthful outcome followed?
5. What would you repeat or improve?

You do not need a perfect story. You need relevant evidence, clearly explained.

For a structured way to prepare your career story, interview examples, and next move, use the link in my bio and select the YOU COACHING SERIES.
