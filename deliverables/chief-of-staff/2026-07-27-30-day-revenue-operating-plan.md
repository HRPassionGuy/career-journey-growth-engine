## Executive ownership

- **VP of Revenue:** Own recognized revenue, the product/price/unit-economics audit, daily qualified lead and reply review, one conversion experiment at a time, weekly funnel requirements, and Friday forecast.
- **Marketing Director:** Own qualified organic distribution, mobile/link-in-bio/storefront reachability, campaign labels where supported, publishing of approved assets, and channel allocation based on downstream evidence.
- **Creative Director:** Reuse YOU COACHING assets and supply objection-led, conversion-oriented assets grounded in verified Research, Community, Revenue, and Analytics evidence.
- **Director of Community:** Run capped, duplicate-safe high-intent engagement; persist exact buyer language and verified actions; release reservations and record a precise blocker when an execution surface requires unavailable action-time confirmation.
- **Director of Analytics:** Maintain the funnel scorecard and denominators; report daily material changes and a Friday variance/forecast with confidence and limitations.
- **Director of Research & Product Development:** Rank buyer-intent signals that can change execution; validate fit before recommending portfolio expansion; improve use of current products first.
- **Director of Automation:** Ensure delivery acknowledgment, claim and heartbeat reliability, terminal result persistence, bounded retry, automatic reservation release, and root-cause classification for every stall.
- **Chief of Staff:** Maintain one accountable owner, reconcile duplicate/stale work, issue daily exception reporting, consolidate weekly progress, and escalate only CEO-reserved decisions.

## KPI hierarchy

### Lagging

- Recognized revenue
- Paid orders
- Average revenue per order
- Product-level revenue
- Revenue per qualified visitor
- Refunds or cancellations when available

### Leading

- Qualified store visits by source
- YOU COACHING SERIES product-page views
- Store-to-product-view rate
- Product-view-to-lead rate
- Product-view-to-order rate
- Qualified leads and lead-to-order rate

- Content-to-product clicks where measurable
- Search impressions and clicks

### Reliability guardrails

- Assignment delivery and claim latency
- Heartbeat age
- Completion rate
- External-action verification rate
- Retry recovery time
- Duplicate-prevention rejections
- Unclassified stalled executions: target 0

## Decision rules

1. Scale only channels and messages that produce verified downstream product views, leads, orders, or revenue.
2. Do not infer conversion performance from traffic volume alone.
3. Preserve one active conversion experiment at a time.
4. Pause or revise activity that produces no downstream movement after a complete comparable period.
5. Disclose small samples, reporting lag, attribution limits, and assumptions.
6. Continue current offers and prices unless the CEO approves a change.

## Critical assumptions

1. Organic channels can create qualified product-page traffic quickly enough; this is unproven.
2. The current product mix and capacity can support the target without a price or offer change; this is unverified.
3. The YOU COACHING SERIES converts when reached; the current sample of 2 views and 0 orders is insufficient.
4. Available attribution can support channel allocation; current source-to-product-to-order lineage is incomplete.
5. Approved assets can be produced and published at the needed cadence; Marcus's recording choice remains a dependency for the two face-to-camera scripts.
6. Executive runtimes can execute daily without silent stalls; the Community incident shows this was not yet reliable.

## CEO-reserved decisions
