## Validation History

Each included standard completed individual validation. The standards then completed integrated architecture validation, correction, and final verification as a unified Phase 2 operating system.

## Completed Integration Corrections

The integrated architecture validation identified and completed three corrections:

1. **Decision-to-initiative handoff:** The Executive Decision Standard and Strategic Initiative Standard were aligned so an approved decision that creates or materially changes a strategic initiative enters the organizational initiative portfolio with an explicit accountable executive and a documented retention or transfer of implementation ownership.
2. **Knowledge ownership and repository governance:** The Organizational Knowledge Standard was clarified so functional executives retain responsibility for the substantive accuracy and completeness of their knowledge assets while the Chief of Staff governs repository acceptance, organization, accessibility, and lifecycle status.
3. **Cross-functional coordination, ownership, and escalation:** The operating standards were aligned so the Chief of Staff coordinates cross-functional matters through the constitutional escalation framework without creating duplicate ownership or assuming another executive's functional responsibilities; every decision and initiative retains one accountable executive.

## Final Validation Status

**Passed.** All six standards passed final verification after completion of the three integration corrections. Each included standard identifies itself as Version 1.0 and is aligned with the Project Constitution and the Phase 2 integrated architecture.

## Freeze Declaration

Phase 2 Operating Standards Version 1.0 is frozen as of July 19, 2026.

Future changes to any included standard require a new version and documented rationale. Changes must follow the Project Constitution and applicable decision authority; they must not be incorporated silently into this frozen release.
