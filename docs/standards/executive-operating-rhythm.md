# Executive Operating Standard

## Executive Operating Rhythm

**Version 1.0**

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

# Purpose

The Executive Operating Rhythm establishes the organization's recurring governance cadence for planning, decision-making, performance review, coordination, and strategic oversight.

Its purpose is to ensure executive activities occur consistently, organizational priorities remain aligned, and leadership maintains an appropriate balance between execution and long-term strategy.

This standard applies to all executives and operates in accordance with the Project Constitution.

---

# Scope

This standard governs:

* Executive coordination.
* Performance reviews.
* Strategic planning.
* Initiative oversight.
* Organizational learning.
* Executive reporting.
* Governance reviews.

It establishes **when** governance activities occur, not **how** they are performed.

---

# Guiding Principles

The Executive Operating Rhythm should:

* Promote organizational alignment.
* Encourage proactive leadership.
* Balance operational and strategic work.
* Create predictable governance cycles.
* Support evidence-based decision-making.
* Reinforce executive accountability.
* Enable continuous organizational improvement.

---

# Weekly Executive Rhythm

The executive team should:

* Review organizational priorities.
* Review strategic initiative progress.
* Review material risks.
* Coordinate cross-functional work.
* Resolve operational dependencies.
* Identify decisions requiring executive attention.

Weekly reviews should emphasize execution and coordination.

---

# Monthly Executive Rhythm

The executive team should:

* Review KPI performance.
* Assess departmental progress.
* Evaluate initiative health.
* Review organizational capacity.
* Confirm resource alignment.
* Adjust short-term priorities where appropriate.

Monthly reviews should emphasize organizational performance.

---

# Quarterly Executive Rhythm

The executive team should:

* Review strategic objectives.
* Evaluate initiative portfolios.
* Assess organizational capability.
* Review executive effectiveness.
* Evaluate major risks.
* Identify strategic opportunities.
* Review significant organizational learning.

Quarterly reviews should emphasize strategic direction and organizational improvement.

---

# Annual Executive Rhythm

The executive team should:

* Review Mission and Vision alignment.
* Evaluate long-term strategic progress.
* Assess organizational maturity.
* Review governance effectiveness.
* Evaluate executive structure.
* Confirm long-term priorities.
* Identify major capability investments.

Annual reviews should emphasize organizational evolution.

---

# Executive Responsibilities

Each executive is responsible for:

* Preparing required information.
* Reporting accurately.
* Identifying emerging risks.
* Recommending improvements.
* Participating in cross-functional governance.
* Supporting executive decisions.

The Chief of Staff coordinates the Executive Operating Rhythm in accordance with the constitutional escalation framework and the Chief of Staff Charter.

---

# Governance Reviews

Recurring governance reviews should include:

* Constitutional compliance.
* Charter effectiveness.
* Operating standard effectiveness.
* Cross-functional collaboration.
* Decision quality.
* Initiative performance.
* Organizational learning.

Recommendations for governance improvements should be documented before implementation. Approval remains subject to the decision authority established by the Project Constitution.

---

# Flexibility

The Executive Operating Rhythm establishes the organization's normal governance cadence.

Extraordinary circumstances may require additional executive reviews or modified timing.

Changes to cadence should support organizational effectiveness without weakening governance discipline.

---

# Continuous Improvement

The executive team should periodically evaluate whether the Executive Operating Rhythm:

* Supports decision quality.
* Encourages collaboration.
* Improves execution.
* Provides sufficient strategic focus.
* Prevents governance bottlenecks.
* Enables organizational learning.

Improvements should be implemented through deliberate governance updates.

---

# Ownership

**Document Owner:** Chief of Staff

The Chief of Staff is responsible for maintaining this standard, coordinating the executive governance cadence, and recommending improvements as organizational needs evolve. Updates remain subject to the Project Constitution and applicable decision authority.
