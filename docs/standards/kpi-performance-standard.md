# Executive Operating Standard

## KPI & Performance Standard

**Version 1.0**

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

# Purpose

The KPI & Performance Standard establishes the organization's common framework for defining, governing, measuring, reporting, and reviewing Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

Its purpose is to ensure organizational performance is measured consistently, supports strategic decision-making, and enables continuous improvement.

This standard applies to all executives and operates in accordance with the Project Constitution.

---

# Scope

This standard applies to:

* Organizational KPIs.
* Departmental KPIs.
* Cross-functional initiatives.
* Executive scorecards.
* Performance reporting.
* Strategic reviews.

Routine operational metrics may be managed within departments, consistent with the applicable executive charter.

---

# Guiding Principles

Performance measurement should be:

* Strategic.
* Objective.
* Evidence-based.
* Actionable.
* Transparent.
* Consistent.
* Comparable over time.

Metrics exist to improve decisions, not merely to generate reports.

---

# KPI Governance

Every KPI shall identify:

* KPI name.
* Business objective supported.
* Definition.
* Calculation method.
* Data source.
* Accountable executive.
* Reporting cadence.
* Target value.
* Review frequency.

Each KPI shall have one accountable executive, consistent with the Project Constitution's requirement for clear accountability.

---

# KPI Categories

## Strategic KPIs

Strategic KPIs measure long-term organizational outcomes.

Examples include:

* Revenue growth.
* Customer success.
* Community growth.
* Product adoption.
* Organizational capability.

## Department KPIs

Department KPIs measure functional performance within an executive's area of responsibility.

Department KPIs remain owned by the accountable executive.

## Initiative KPIs

Initiative KPIs measure the success of strategic initiatives.

Ownership follows the [Strategic Initiative Standard](strategic-initiative-standard.md).

---

# Performance Reporting

Executive performance reports should include:

* KPI status.
* Trends.
* Significant changes.
* Contributing factors.
* Risks.
* Corrective actions.
* Decisions required.

Reports should focus on meaningful performance changes rather than isolated numbers and should follow the [Executive Communication Standard](executive-communication-standard.md).

---

# KPI Ownership

The accountable executive is responsible for:

* Business objectives supported.
* Target values.
* Interpretation.
* Corrective actions.
* Resulting performance.

The Director of Analytics is responsible for:

* KPI definitions.
* Measurement methodology.
* Measurement quality.
* Data quality standards.
* Reporting standards and consistency.
* Executive dashboard governance.

The Director of Analytics has final authority over KPI definitions, methodology, measurement quality, and reporting standards, consistent with the Director of Analytics Charter.

Analytics governs measurement.

Executives govern performance.

---

# Performance Review

KPIs should be reviewed according to the Executive Operating Rhythm and the planning and review cadence established by the Project Constitution.

Reviews should evaluate:

* Progress toward targets.
* Trends.
* Risks.
* Strategic implications.
* Improvement opportunities.

---

# KPI Changes

Material KPI changes should document:

* Reason for change.
* Strategic rationale.
* Impact on historical comparisons.
* Executive approval where appropriate.

KPIs should remain stable enough to support meaningful trend analysis. Approval follows the decision authority established by the Project Constitution and the [Executive Decision Standard](executive-decision-standard.md).

---

# Continuous Improvement

Executives should periodically evaluate whether KPIs:

* Support strategic objectives.
* Drive appropriate behaviors.
* Enable better decisions.
* Reflect customer outcomes.
* Remain relevant.

Metrics that no longer support organizational learning should be revised or retired.

---

# Ownership

**Document Owner:** Director of Analytics

The Director of Analytics is responsible for maintaining this standard, ensuring consistent measurement practices, and recommending improvements as organizational needs evolve. Updates remain subject to the Project Constitution and applicable decision authority.

# Relationship to Other Standards

This standard complements:

* The [Executive Decision Standard](executive-decision-standard.md).
* The [Strategic Initiative Standard](strategic-initiative-standard.md).
* The [Executive Communication Standard](executive-communication-standard.md).
