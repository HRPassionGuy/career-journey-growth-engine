# COS-003: Weekly Executive Review

**Procedure ID:** COS-003  
**Procedure Name:** Weekly Executive Review  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner / Accountable Executive:** Chief of Staff  
**Approver:** CEO  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-19  
**Review Frequency:** Quarterly and upon a material governance trigger

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Run the weekly execution-and-coordination control cycle so current priorities, initiative milestones, dependencies, risks, decisions, and assignments remain visible and owned. The outcome is timely blocker resolution and reliable 0–30 day execution; the impact is less drift, duplication, rework, and communication delay.

## Scope

Applies to the weekly executive cadence and extraordinary weekly-level review called for urgent coordination. It does not replace functional operating meetings, monthly KPI/organizational performance review, quarterly strategy/portfolio decisions, or CEO approval.

## Trigger

**Scheduled trigger:** the recurring weekly executive review calendar event and its submission cutoff.  
**Event-driven triggers:** urgent portfolio-wide dependency, material operational risk, CEO request, or a missed review requiring rescheduling.

## Preconditions

* The current initiative portfolio, decision register, risk register, prior action log, and executive calendar are accessible.
* Each executive has a named reporting deadline and current reporting template.
* Director of Analytics has published or validated the current performance exceptions used in the review.
* Agenda time and attendance or authorized delegates are confirmed.

## Required Inputs

| Input | Source or Owner | Quality or Approval Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Executive update | Each executive | Priorities, material change, milestones, risks, decisions, capacity, next commitment; submitted by cutoff |
| Initiative status | Accountable executives | Current milestone, variance, dependency, risk, and next milestone against portfolio baseline |
| Performance exception snapshot | Director of Analytics | Current Analytics-governed definitions, sources, trends, limitations |
| Open decisions/actions/risks | Chief of Staff | Current status, owner, due date, escalation level, and source link |
| Prior review record | Chief of Staff | Unclosed assignments and decision follow-ups reconciled |

| RACI role | Participant | Responsibility | Required Input | Expected Output or Handoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Responsible / Accountable | Chief of Staff | Prepare, conduct, time-box, record, route, and close the review | Complete weekly packet | Accepted record, assignments, escalations, updates |

| Consulted | Director of Analytics | Validate measures and material performance interpretation limits | Dashboard snapshot | Measurement-quality statement |
| Informed | CEO | Receive reserved decisions, significant risk, and concise weekly briefing | Escalation/briefing | Decision or acknowledgment |

## Procedure Steps

1. **Collect and quality-control weekly submissions**
   * **Objective:** Build a current, exception-based operating record before the session.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff; executives own substantive accuracy.
   * **Required inputs:** All required weekly inputs.
   * **Action:** Reconcile submissions to portfolio, register, dashboard, risk, and prior actions; flag stale, missing, contradictory, duplicate, or unvalidated entries; return deficiencies with owner and cure deadline.
   * **Decision point:** Proceed with a disclosed nonblocking gap; defer an item whose evidence cannot support action; escalate repeated non-reporting.
   * **Expected output:** Validated weekly packet and exception list.
   * **Evidence or record:** Submission log, validation status, limitations, and timestamps.
   * **Handoff:** Accepted packet proceeds to agenda construction.
2. **Prioritize the decision-and-exception agenda**
   * **Objective:** Use review time on changes requiring coordination or authority.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff.
   * **Required inputs:** Validated packet and time available.
   * **Action:** Order items by customer/mission impact, urgency, risk, milestone variance, dependency criticality, decision deadline, and opportunity cost; assign each item an owner, desired output, time box, and authority path.
   * **Decision point:** Move informational items to the written briefing; route functional items directly to owners; place reserved items in a CEO decision section.
   * **Expected output:** Distributed agenda and pre-read.
   * **Evidence or record:** Agenda version, distribution time, and item disposition.
   * **Handoff:** Participants acknowledge required preparation.
3. **Reconcile prior commitments**
   * **Objective:** Close, revise, or escalate every due action before new assignments are created.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff facilitates; assigned executives report evidence.
   * **Required inputs:** Prior action log and completion evidence.
   * **Action:** For each due item, record completed with acceptance evidence, continuing with reason/new date, blocked with dependency, or escalated with required decision.
   * **Decision point:** Material scope or priority change invokes COS-001; cross-functional blocker invokes COS-006.
   * **Expected output:** Reconciled action log.
   * **Evidence or record:** Status, evidence link, owner, due date, and approved change.
   * **Handoff:** Unresolved items enter the appropriate agenda section.
4. **Review priorities, initiatives, dependencies, and risks**
   * **Objective:** Protect the current operating horizon and surface exceptions early.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff coordinates; each accountable executive owns its item.
   * **Required inputs:** Agenda, portfolio status, performance exceptions, risk register.
   * **Action:** Compare current state to milestone, success criteria, capacity, and approved priority; identify variance cause, customer/organizational impact, corrective recommendation, dependency owner, and required date. Record only decisions, changes, risks, and commitments—not general conversation.
   * **Decision point:** Functional correction returns to that executive; cross-functional conflict uses COS-006; CEO-reserved matter uses COS-001; new initiative uses COS-002 after approval.
   * **Expected output:** Confirmed status, corrective assignments, escalation referrals.
   * **Evidence or record:** Item-level outcome and source links.
   * **Handoff:** Owners explicitly accept each assignment.
5. **Confirm decisions, assignments, and communications**
   * **Objective:** End with unambiguous accountable action.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff.
   * **Required inputs:** Recorded item outcomes.
   * **Action:** Read back each decision/referral and assignment with one owner, output, acceptance condition, deadline, dependency, approver, and next checkpoint; identify affected informed parties.
   * **Decision point:** Do not accept shared ownership or an assignment outside a charter; resolve through COS-006 or CEO route.
   * **Expected output:** Accepted action and decision-routing log.
   * **Evidence or record:** Owner acknowledgments and unresolved objection record.
   * **Handoff:** Owners receive assignments; CEO receives reserved items.
6. **Publish and update governing records**
   * **Objective:** Make the review auditable and synchronize organizational systems.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff; Analytics owns dashboard methods.
   * **Required inputs:** Final record and acknowledgments.
   * **Action:** Publish the executive summary; update action, decision, initiative, risk, and knowledge records; request Analytics-governed dashboard changes; distribute audience-specific communication within one business day.
   * **Decision point:** Restrict confidential items while preserving authorized records.
   * **Expected output:** Synchronized records and CEO weekly briefing.
   * **Evidence or record:** Repository links, change log, recipients, and delivery timestamps.
   * **Handoff:** COS-004 receives accumulated monthly trends; COS-005 receives strategic signals.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agenda priority, time allocation, and coordination sequence | Impact, urgency, risk, dependencies, deadlines | Chief of Staff | None within delegated authority | Weekly agenda |
| Functional corrective action | Charter authority and domain evidence | Functional executive | None unless reserved | Action log |
| Cross-functional resolution | Accepted commitments within existing authority | Relevant accountable executive; Chief of Staff coordinates | Applicable authority | COS-006 or action |
| Reserved strategic/material decision | COS-001 package | CEO | CEO | Decision record |

## Decision Rules

Chief of Staff may set agenda, deadlines, reporting format, facilitation sequence, request analysis, assign work to the proper executive, recommend sequencing, and delay nonessential coordination. CEO approval remains required for reserved matters. Functional executives retain domain decisions and outcomes; Analytics retains measurement authority. Escalate missing ownership, repeated missed commitments, cross-functional dependency conflicts, significant risks, policy conflicts, and any reserved matter.

## Escalation Conditions

| Condition | Escalation Level | Escalation Recipient | Required Information | Execution Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Domain correction only | Department | Functional executive | Variance, cause, action, date | Continue under owner |
| Cross-functional blocker/conflicting commitments | Cross-functional | Chief of Staff via COS-006 | Owners, impact, options, urgency | Continue safe work; pause contested work |
| Significant risk/reserved choice | Executive | CEO via COS-001 | Decision brief | Pause affected commitment |
| Stale/missing material reporting | Cross-functional | Chief of Staff; CEO if repeated/material | Missing record, impact, cure attempts | Disclose limitation; withhold unsupported action |

## Success Criteria

* Every due commitment and material exception has an evidence-backed disposition.
* Every new action has one charter-correct owner, output, acceptance condition, and deadline.
* Required escalations and record updates are issued within one business day.
* Informational reporting does not displace decision, risk, or dependency work.

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may collect submissions, validate fields/links, identify changes and overdue actions, draft agendas and summaries, route assignments, and synchronize registers. Humans retain all reserved approvals, risk acceptance, functional judgments, target ownership, and substantive validation. Integrations may include calendars, communications, dashboard, portfolio, decision/action/risk registers, and Knowledge Repository. Automation must not fabricate status, infer consent, change priorities or business logic, redefine KPIs, or close work without acceptance evidence.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Quarterly during COS-007.
* **Event-based review triggers:** repeated missed inputs/actions, control failure, cadence change, material communication failure, governance change, or automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** Chief of Staff.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Director of Analytics, participating executives, CEO for reserved changes.
