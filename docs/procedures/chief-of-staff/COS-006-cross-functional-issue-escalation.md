# COS-006: Cross-functional Issue Escalation

**Procedure ID:** COS-006  
**Procedure Name:** Cross-functional Issue Escalation  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner / Accountable Executive:** Chief of Staff  
**Approver:** CEO  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-19  
**Review Frequency:** Quarterly and upon a material governance trigger

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Resolve cross-functional ownership, dependency, priority, evidence, capacity, and execution conflicts through a time-bound, authority-correct path. The outcome is an accepted resolution or CEO-ready escalation; the impact is reduced delay without weakening functional accountability.

## Scope

Applies after a matter spans two or more functions and cannot be resolved by the functional owner alone. It does not transfer functional work to the Chief of Staff, override charter authority, substitute for COS-001 on reserved decisions, or manage routine departmental issues.

## Trigger

**Scheduled triggers:** unresolved issue identified in COS-003, COS-004, or COS-005; open escalation reaches checkpoint.  
**Event-driven triggers:** executive submits an escalation; owner/dependency dispute; conflicting priorities; material cross-functional blockage; risk or communication failure requiring coordinated action.

## Preconditions

* The submitting executive states the attempted functional resolution and why it failed.
* The issue has a proposed accountable owner, affected executives, impact, urgency, and requested outcome.
* Safe reversible work is distinguished from contested or risky work.
* Source records and applicable approvals are accessible.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Register, contain, and classify the issue**
   * **Objective:** Protect the organization while establishing scope and authority.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff.
   * **Required inputs:** Escalation brief and source records.
   * **Action:** Assign ID/time; classify owner, affected functions, issue type, urgency, impact, authority, and safe/contested work; search for duplicate issues; require proportionate temporary containment from the functional owner.
   * **Decision point:** Return department-only matters; combine duplicates; immediately route significant risk or reserved matters to CEO through COS-001.
   * **Expected output:** Classified escalation and containment state.
   * **Evidence or record:** Intake, classification rationale, linked records, temporary controls.
   * **Handoff:** Affected executives acknowledge within the stated response window.
2. **Validate facts, authority, and positions**
   * **Objective:** Separate evidence disagreement from ownership or priority conflict.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff coordinates; executives validate substance; Analytics validates measures.
   * **Required inputs:** Governance, affected positions, evidence.
   * **Action:** Map charter authority and prior approvals; reconcile facts, assumptions, limitations, dependencies, constraints, and decision rights; document agreement and disagreement.
   * **Decision point:** Governance conflict invokes COS-007; measurement dispute goes to Analytics; missing material evidence produces a named evidence action and deadline.
   * **Expected output:** Validated issue map.
   * **Evidence or record:** Authority/evidence matrix and disagreement log.
   * **Handoff:** Participants accept their recorded position.
3. **Develop resolution options**
   * **Objective:** Produce feasible choices with explicit trade-offs.
   * **Responsible:** Accountable executive proposes; Chief of Staff synthesizes; affected executives contribute.
   * **Required inputs:** Issue map, constraints, risk/capacity evidence.
   * **Action:** Define at least one compliant option and, when practical, alternatives; for each specify owner, deliverables, dependencies, resource/schedule impact, customer impact, risks, reversibility, success criteria, and authority.
   * **Decision point:** Remove options that violate governance or require unavailable authority; disclose when only containment is feasible.
   * **Expected output:** Resolution recommendation.
   * **Evidence or record:** Options, rationale, dissent, confidence.
   * **Handoff:** Proper decision owner receives package.
4. **Obtain and record resolution**
   * **Objective:** Secure an explicit decision from the correct authority.
   * **Responsible:** Functional decision owner or CEO decides; Chief of Staff facilitates/records.
   * **Required inputs:** Recommendation and authority map.
   * **Action:** Record approve, reject, defer, or request evidence; capture conditions, accepted risks, one accountable owner, supporting commitments, output, acceptance criteria, deadlines, communications, and review date.
   * **Decision point:** Chief of Staff may coordinate a resolution within existing delegated authorities but may not decide domain substance; unresolved or reserved conflict goes through COS-001 to CEO.
   * **Expected output:** Accepted resolution or CEO decision request.
   * **Evidence or record:** Resolution record, acknowledgments, approval links.
   * **Handoff:** Action owners accept; CEO package goes to COS-001.
5. **Monitor closure and publish learning**
   * **Objective:** Verify the issue is resolved, not merely reassigned.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff monitors; action owners execute/validate.
   * **Required inputs:** Resolution commitments and evidence.
   * **Action:** Check outputs at agreed dates; confirm dependency acceptance, risk status, and success criteria; reopen or escalate failed resolution; update source records and publish validated learning.
   * **Decision point:** Material scope/initiative change uses COS-001 then COS-002; repeated systemic issue enters COS-007 or COS-008.
   * **Expected output:** Closed escalation, synchronized records, learning asset.
   * **Evidence or record:** Acceptance evidence, closure rationale, lessons, communications.
   * **Handoff:** COS-003/004 monitors resulting actions; COS-007/008 receives systemic findings.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Classification, containment coordination, deadline | Impact, urgency, charter map | Chief of Staff | None within delegated authority | Return, coordinate, or escalate |
| Functional substance and commitments | Charter authority and evidence | Relevant functional executive | None unless reserved | Resolution action |
| KPI definition/method dispute | Measurement evidence | Director of Analytics | None within measurement authority | Measurement determination |
| Reserved/unresolved material conflict | COS-001 package | CEO | CEO | Final direction |

## Decision Rules

Chief of Staff may classify, convene, request analysis, recommend sequencing, set response dates, coordinate containment, consolidate options, assign work to the proper executive, and delay nonessential contested work. CEO approval is required for reserved matters and final unresolved material conflicts. Functional executives retain domain authority; Analytics retains measurement authority. Escalate when significant risk, mission/strategy, material investment/resource, pricing, structure, policy conflict, missed containment, or inability to name one owner exists.

## Escalation Conditions

| Condition | Escalation Level | Escalation Recipient | Required Information | Execution Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single-function issue | Department | Functional executive | Classification, evidence, due date | Return and track only if material |
| Multi-function issue within delegated authorities | Cross-functional | Chief of Staff | Complete issue map | Coordinate resolution |
| Reserved/unresolved/significant-risk issue | Executive | CEO via COS-001 | Recommendation, dissent, risks, deadline | Pause contested commitment |
| Policy/governance conflict | Executive | COS-007 then CEO if required | Conflicting sources and options | Stop noncompliant path |

| Output | Owner | Recipient or Repository | Acceptance or Completion Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Escalation/containment record | Chief of Staff | Escalation/risk records | ID, authority, owners, impact, controls complete |
| Issue map and resolution options | Substantive owners / Chief of Staff | Decision owner | Evidence, positions, trade-offs, confidence complete |
| Resolution/CEO package | Proper decision owner | Action owners or COS-001 | Explicit decision and approvals |
| Closure and learning record | Chief of Staff / functional owners | Knowledge Repository | Success criteria verified and learning validated |

## Success Criteria

* Correct authority receives the issue within the required response window.
* Resolution has one accountable owner, accepted dependencies, output, deadline, and verification evidence.
* Contested or unsafe work does not proceed without authority.
* Repeated systemic issues feed COS-007 or COS-008 and source records are synchronized.

## Risks and Controls

| Risk | Impact | Control | Control Owner | Required Evidence | Escalation Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chief of Staff overrides function | Authority conflict | Charter map and substantive decision owner | Chief of Staff | Authority matrix | Any disputed override |
| Unsafe delay | Customer/organizational harm | Time-based triage and containment | Functional owner / Chief of Staff | Containment record | Missed critical response |
| Hidden disagreement | Fragile resolution | Position/dissent log | Chief of Staff | Acknowledged issue map | Material unrecorded dissent |
| Missing approval | Unauthorized resolution | COS-001 gate | Chief of Staff | Approval link | Any reserved change |
| Communication failure | Continued conflict | Named recipients and acknowledgments | Chief of Staff | Communication log | Affected owner uninformed |

## Exceptions

| Permitted Exception | Authorizing Role | Conditions and Limits | Required Documentation | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Emergency containment | Existing functional authority; CEO if reserved | Reversible/proportionate; no authority expansion | Risk, action, owner, expiry | Retrospective review promptly |
| Executive unavailable | Chief of Staff for process | Delegate or documented gap; no authority transfer | Delegation/limitation | Ratification |
| Incomplete evidence | Correct decision authority | Delay is riskier; confidence/gaps explicit | Provisional resolution and review date | Evidence completion |

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may classify issues, detect duplicates, collect positions, track response windows, draft option matrices, route communications, and update records. Humans retain substantive domain decisions, risk acceptance, conflict judgment, ownership commitments, and CEO approvals. Integrations may include registers, portfolio, repository, dashboard, calendars, and messaging. Automation must not override a charter, infer resolution/consent, suppress dissent, or expand containment into permanent business logic.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Quarterly during COS-007.
* **Event-based review triggers:** repeated recurrence, missed response, authority dispute, control failure, governance change, or automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** Chief of Staff.
* **Supporting reviewers:** affected executives, Director of Analytics, CEO for reserved changes.
