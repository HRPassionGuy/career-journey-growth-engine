# COS-001: Executive Decision Facilitation

**Procedure ID:** COS-001  
**Procedure Name:** Executive Decision Facilitation  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner / Accountable Executive:** Chief of Staff  
**Approver:** CEO  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-19  
**Review Frequency:** Quarterly and upon a material governance trigger

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

This procedure converts a significant decision request into a complete, authority-correct, evidence-based decision and an auditable implementation handoff. The business outcome is faster, higher-quality executive decisions with explicit ownership, approvals, risks, and review dates. Its organizational impact is reduced ambiguity, fewer unauthorized commitments, and reusable decision learning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to material department, cross-functional, and CEO-reserved decisions that require structured executive facilitation. It does not apply to routine operational choices within a functional executive's charter, informal conversations, or the implementation work created by an approved decision.

## Trigger

**Scheduled triggers:** an unresolved decision identified in COS-003, COS-004, or COS-005; a decision review date recorded in the decision register.  
**Event-driven triggers:** an executive submits a material decision request; COS-002 identifies a required approval; COS-006 escalates an issue requiring a decision; COS-007 or COS-008 identifies a corrective decision.

## Preconditions

* The decision request has one proposed decision owner and a clearly stated decision deadline.
* The functional owner has exhausted routine authority before requesting cross-functional or CEO involvement.
* Relevant records are accessible and confidential or sensitive information is handled under applicable ethical and data obligations.
* Urgent incomplete-information decisions are explicitly identified as exceptions before Step 3.

## Required Inputs

| Input | Source or Owner | Quality or Approval Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Decision request | Proposed decision owner | States situation, requested decision, urgency, affected functions, and desired date |
| Decision brief | Decision owner | Separates facts, observations, assumptions, interpretations, opinions, limitations, and confidence |
| Alternatives and impact analysis | Decision owner; consulted executives | Includes strategic alignment, customer value, capacity, resources, risks, trade-offs, expected outcomes, and opportunity cost |
| KPI and dashboard evidence | Director of Analytics | Uses current Analytics-governed definitions and discloses data limitations |
| Applicable governance | Chief of Staff | Current Constitution, AGENTS.md, charters, standards, accepted ADRs, and active procedures |
| Functional recommendations | Consulted executives | Validated within each executive's charter and identifies agreement or disagreement |

## Participants and Responsibilities

| RACI role | Participant | Responsibility | Required Input | Expected Output or Handoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Responsible | Chief of Staff | Classify, coordinate, quality-control, route, record, and communicate the decision | Decision request and governance | Decision-ready package and traceable record |
| Accountable | Chief of Staff | Ensure procedure integrity without making reserved or functional decisions | Complete execution record | Accepted decision record and handoffs |
| Consulted | Decision owner and affected functional executives | Supply substantive analysis and charter-specific recommendations | Domain evidence | Validated recommendation, risks, and commitments |
| Consulted | Director of Analytics | Validate KPI definitions, methods, data quality, and reporting consistency | Measurement evidence | Measurement validation or documented limitation |
| Informed | CEO, implementation owner, supporting executives | Receive the package, decision, or implementation assignment appropriate to authority | Approved communication | Acknowledgment or escalation |

## Procedure Steps

1. **Register and classify the request**
   * **Objective:** Establish traceability, materiality, authority level, and timing before analysis begins.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff.
   * **Required inputs:** Decision request; applicable charter; known deadline and impact.
   * **Action:** Assign a decision-register identifier; classify the matter as department, cross-functional, or executive; name one decision owner, supporting executives, proposed implementation owner, and required approver. Reject duplicate requests by linking them to the existing open record.
   * **Decision point:** Route routine department decisions back to the functional executive; retain cross-functional coordination; route CEO-reserved matters to the CEO.
   * **Expected output:** Classified decision intake record.
   * **Evidence or record:** Register entry with status, authority rationale, owners, trigger, and due date.
   * **Handoff:** Decision owner accepts the classification or provides a documented conflict for Step 2.
2. **Validate authority and decision readiness**
   * **Objective:** Prevent unauthorized decision-making and incomplete recommendations.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff; decision owner validates substance.
   * **Required inputs:** Classified record, governance sources, decision brief.
   * **Action:** Map the requested decision to constitutional and charter authority; verify the brief contains situation, evidence, analysis, recommendation, risks, decision required, confidence, alternatives, expected outcomes, success measures, and review date.
   * **Decision point:** Return incomplete packages with named deficiencies and a resubmission time; pause any request that conflicts with higher governance.
   * **Expected output:** Readiness determination and authority map.
   * **Evidence or record:** Completed readiness record with source links and unresolved gaps.
   * **Handoff:** Ready package proceeds to Step 3; governance conflict proceeds to COS-007.
3. **Obtain bounded executive contributions**
   * **Objective:** Produce a cross-functional evidence base without creating shared accountability.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff coordinates; affected executives produce their domain inputs.
   * **Required inputs:** Readiness determination and specific contribution requests.
   * **Action:** Issue each consulted executive a request stating the question, evidence needed, output format, deadline, and boundary. Require the Director of Analytics to validate material measurement inputs and require dissent to state evidence, trade-offs, and an alternative.
   * **Decision point:** If a required executive is unavailable, use an authorized delegate or record the missing perspective and assess whether the decision may proceed.
   * **Expected output:** Validated contributions and a disagreement log.
   * **Evidence or record:** Dated submissions, validation status, limitations, and confidence.
   * **Handoff:** Chief of Staff accepts contributions that meet the request.
4. **Synthesize the decision package**
   * **Objective:** Give the decision maker a concise, complete basis for action.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff.
   * **Required inputs:** Accepted contributions, authority map, measurement validation.
   * **Action:** Produce one package using the Executive Communication Standard: situation, evidence, analysis, recommendation, risks, decision required, and confidence. Show agreement, disagreement, alternatives, opportunity cost, capacity, dependencies, implementation owner, success measures, and decision/review dates.
   * **Decision point:** Escalate unresolvable evidence conflicts, significant risk, or a CEO-reserved choice; do not resolve domain substance on behalf of a functional executive.
   * **Expected output:** Decision-ready executive brief.
   * **Evidence or record:** Versioned brief linked to all supporting evidence.
   * **Handoff:** Required decision maker confirms receipt and decision deadline.
5. **Facilitate and record the decision**
   * **Objective:** Obtain an explicit result from the correct authority.
   * **Responsible:** Decision maker decides; Chief of Staff facilitates and records.
   * **Required inputs:** Decision-ready brief and any time-critical updates.
   * **Action:** Present the precise decision requested; capture approve, reject, defer, or request-more-evidence; record conditions, accepted risks, dissent, owner, implementation date, success measures, and review date.
   * **Decision point:** A deferral must name missing evidence, its owner, and resubmission date; conditional approval must state each condition and verifier.
   * **Expected output:** Explicit decision and approval record.
   * **Evidence or record:** Completed executive decision register entry and linked approval evidence.
   * **Handoff:** Approved decisions proceed to Step 6; rejected or deferred records proceed to Step 7.
6. **Create the implementation or initiative handoff**
   * **Objective:** Translate approval into accountable execution without transferring functional ownership improperly.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff; implementation owner accepts.
   * **Required inputs:** Approved decision and conditions.
   * **Action:** Confirm one implementation owner, contributors, outputs, dependencies, milestones, decision conditions, measures, and reporting cadence. If the decision creates or materially changes a strategic initiative, initiate COS-002 and update the organizational initiative portfolio only after its acceptance controls pass.
   * **Decision point:** Escalate a disputed owner or resource conflict through COS-006; route material scope, investment, pricing, structure, risk, mission, or priority changes to the CEO.
   * **Expected output:** Accepted implementation assignment or COS-002 intake.
   * **Evidence or record:** Dated acceptance, portfolio link where applicable, and communications log.
   * **Handoff:** Accountable executive confirms ownership and first milestone.
7. **Publish, communicate, and schedule review**
   * **Objective:** Close the decision cycle and preserve organizational learning.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff.
   * **Required inputs:** Final decision record and handoff state.
   * **Action:** Publish the accepted decision record in the Knowledge Repository; send audience-specific communication stating decision, rationale, owner, effective date, conditions, and next checkpoint; place the review date in the executive cadence.
   * **Decision point:** Restrict distribution when confidentiality requires it while preserving the authorized audit record.
   * **Expected output:** Published record, acknowledged communication, and scheduled review.
   * **Evidence or record:** Repository acceptance, distribution list, acknowledgments, and review-calendar entry.
   * **Handoff:** COS-003, COS-004, or COS-005 receives the review item according to its horizon.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Authority classification and facilitation method | Charter scope, functions affected, materiality, urgency | Chief of Staff | None within delegated coordination authority | Department return, cross-functional facilitation, or CEO route |
| Functional recommendation | Domain evidence and charter authority | Relevant functional executive | None unless higher authority is triggered | Included as owner recommendation |
| Strategic direction, major investment, pricing, structure, significant risk, mission alignment, or final executive choice | Complete decision package | CEO | CEO | Approve, reject, defer, or request evidence |
| Create or materially change an initiative | Approved decision and Strategic Initiative Standard criteria | Accountable executive | Applicable decision authority | COS-002 intake |

## Decision Rules

The Chief of Staff may classify requests, set facilitation deadlines, request analysis, recommend sequencing, consolidate recommendations, return incomplete packages, and delay nonessential coordination work when capacity is constrained. The CEO must approve reserved decisions and any governance change requiring executive direction. Functional executives decide matters inside their charters and own their recommendations, commitments, targets, corrective actions, and outcomes. Escalation is mandatory for disputed authority, unresolved cross-functional conflict, policy conflict, significant risk, material resource or priority conflict, mission concern, or inability to name one accountable owner.

## Escalation Conditions

| Condition | Escalation Level | Escalation Recipient | Required Information | Execution Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Domain choice remains within one charter | Department | Functional executive | Request, evidence, deadline | Chief of Staff records return; functional executive decides |
| Ownership, dependency, capacity, or evidence conflict spans functions | Cross-functional | Chief of Staff via COS-006 | Impact, positions, options, urgency | Continue safe work; pause contested commitments |
| Reserved authority, strategic conflict, or significant risk | Executive | CEO | Full decision brief and recommendation | Pause affected commitment until CEO decision |
| Higher-governance conflict | Executive | CEO after COS-007 analysis | Conflicting sources, impact, compliant options | Stop conflicting path |

## Success Criteria

* The correct authority makes an explicit, documented decision by the required date.
* Every approved decision has one implementation owner, success measures, conditions, and review date.
* Initiative-creating decisions enter COS-002 once and no unauthorized or duplicate portfolio record is created.
* Affected executives receive and acknowledge the decision before execution begins.

## Risks and Controls

| Risk | Impact | Control | Control Owner | Required Evidence | Escalation Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unauthorized decision | Invalid commitment | Authority map and approver verification before facilitation | Chief of Staff | Governance citations and approval record | Any mismatch |
| Missing or biased evidence | Poor decision | Readiness gate, evidence classification, dissent and confidence capture | Decision owner | Validated brief | Material gap or undisclosed limitation |
| Skipped approval | Unenforceable action | No handoff without linked approval evidence | Chief of Staff | Decision-register approval field | Any missing reserved approval |
| Duplicate decision or initiative | Conflicting work | Unique-register search before creation | Chief of Staff | Search/link record | Any probable duplicate |
| Communication failure | Misaligned execution | Named recipients, acknowledgments, and effective-date control | Chief of Staff | Communication log | Owner does not acknowledge before start |

## Exceptions

| Permitted Exception | Authorizing Role | Conditions and Limits | Required Documentation | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Urgent decision with incomplete information | Existing decision authority; CEO if reserved | Delay creates greater risk; authority is unchanged | Known facts, gaps, risk of delay, provisional decision, owner | Complete record as soon as practical and review at next cadence |
| Required executive unavailable | Chief of Staff for process; decision authority unchanged | Authorized delegate or documented missing perspective; no reserved approval substitution | Availability, delegation, limitation | Validate with returning executive when material |
| Confidential distribution | Decision owner within applicable obligations | Limit audience only; preserve authorized record | Access rationale and recipient list | Review access at decision review |

## Future Automation Considerations

AI assistance may validate required fields, detect duplicate records, classify evidence, draft decision briefs, route contribution requests, monitor deadlines, and prepare repository and dashboard updates. Human approval remains required for CEO-reserved decisions, acceptance of significant risk, governance changes, and any approval the source authority reserves. Potential integrations include the decision register, initiative portfolio, Knowledge Repository, executive calendar, communication channels, and Analytics-governed dashboard. Automation must not infer approval, select business strategy, override functional recommendations, alter KPI definitions, or change procedure logic.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Quarterly during COS-007.
* **Event-based review triggers:** Authority error, skipped approval, repeated exception, material decision failure, governance change, or automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** Chief of Staff.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Director of Analytics for measurement; affected functional executives for their responsibilities; CEO for reserved-authority changes.
