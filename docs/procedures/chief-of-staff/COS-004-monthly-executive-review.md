# COS-004: Monthly Executive Review

**Procedure ID:** COS-004  
**Procedure Name:** Monthly Executive Review  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner / Accountable Executive:** Chief of Staff  
**Approver:** CEO  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-19  
**Review Frequency:** Quarterly and upon a material governance trigger

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Execute the monthly organizational-performance cycle by converting KPI trends, departmental progress, initiative health, capacity, and resource evidence into coordinated corrective actions and properly authorized decisions. The outcome is aligned 30–90 day execution and the impact is earlier correction of performance gaps and resource misalignment.

## Scope

Applies to the monthly executive cadence and an extraordinary month-level performance review. It does not redefine KPIs, replace weekly coordination or quarterly strategy, conduct functional analysis for an executive, or approve CEO-reserved changes.

## Trigger

**Scheduled trigger:** monthly review calendar event and reporting cutoff.  
**Event-driven triggers:** material performance variance, portfolio deterioration, capacity shock, CEO request, or a missed monthly review.

## Preconditions

* The Director of Analytics has published a validated monthly dashboard and limitations statement.
* Executives have submitted departmental and initiative narratives by the cutoff.
* Weekly records, decision/initiative/risk registers, capacity view, and prior monthly actions are current.
* The agenda identifies expected decisions, authority, and attendees or delegates.

## Required Inputs

| Input | Source or Owner | Quality or Approval Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly dashboard and KPI exception report | Director of Analytics | Governed definitions/methods; trends, causes, limitations, and data quality stated |
| Department progress and corrective recommendations | Each functional executive | Objectives, target interpretation, corrective action, risks, decisions, and 30–90 day outlook |
| Initiative health and capacity view | Accountable executives; Chief of Staff | Baselines, milestones, resources, dependencies, risks, opportunity cost |
| Weekly roll-up and prior actions | Chief of Staff | Reconciled changes, decisions, escalations, and completion evidence |
| Customer/research evidence where material | Community; Research & Product Development; other owner | Validated by substantive owner and linked to source |

## Procedure Steps

1. **Assemble and validate the monthly evidence pack**
   * **Objective:** Establish one reconciled organizational performance view.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff coordinates; Analytics and executives validate their content.
   * **Required inputs:** All monthly inputs.
   * **Action:** Reconcile dashboard trends with department narratives, initiative baselines, weekly changes, capacity, risks, and prior actions; return contradictions or gaps to their substantive owners with a cure time.
   * **Decision point:** Disclose nonblocking limitations; defer unsupported action; escalate material data-quality failure.
   * **Expected output:** Validated evidence pack and exception map.
   * **Evidence or record:** Source, owner, currency, validation, limitation, and correction log.
   * **Handoff:** Accepted pack to Step 2.
2. **Construct the performance-and-decision agenda**
   * **Objective:** Prioritize material changes and 30–90 day choices.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff.
   * **Required inputs:** Evidence pack, open decisions, capacity constraints.
   * **Action:** Rank items by mission/customer impact, target variance, trend persistence, initiative risk, resource pressure, decision deadline, and opportunity cost; state owner, desired output, evidence, and authority route.
   * **Decision point:** Written-information items leave the live agenda; weekly blockers route to COS-003/COS-006; strategic portfolio issues queue for COS-005 unless urgency requires COS-001.
   * **Expected output:** Agenda and pre-read.
   * **Evidence or record:** Prioritization rationale and distribution log.
   * **Handoff:** Executives acknowledge preparation.
3. **Evaluate KPI and departmental performance exceptions**
   * **Objective:** Convert meaningful performance changes into owner-led action.
   * **Responsible:** Each functional executive owns interpretation/action; Analytics owns measurement; Chief of Staff coordinates.
   * **Required inputs:** KPI exceptions and departmental narratives.
   * **Action:** For each material variance, record trend, cause evidence, uncertainty, customer/strategic implication, current action, recommended correction, owner, target impact, and decision required.
   * **Decision point:** KPI definition/method issues return to Analytics; target/corrective action remains with accountable executive; reserved changes invoke COS-001.
   * **Expected output:** Performance disposition by exception.
   * **Evidence or record:** Accepted analysis, correction, date, and confidence.
   * **Handoff:** Actions and decisions to Step 5.
4. **Evaluate initiative health, capacity, and resource alignment**
   * **Objective:** Keep the initiative portfolio executable and aligned.
   * **Responsible:** Accountable initiative executives; Chief of Staff coordinates.
   * **Required inputs:** Initiative/capacity view, dependencies, risks.
   * **Action:** Compare milestones, measures, resources, dependencies, risks, and completion horizon to baseline; identify saturation, duplication, conflicts, and proposed sequence or scope response.
   * **Decision point:** Cross-functional conflicts use COS-006; material initiative changes use COS-001 then COS-002; strategic reprioritization requires CEO.
   * **Expected output:** Health disposition and portfolio recommendations.
   * **Evidence or record:** Variances, owner response, resource impact, and route.
   * **Handoff:** Accepted adjustments or decision requests to Step 5.
5. **Confirm decisions and 30–90 day commitments**
   * **Objective:** Establish explicit accountable action and escalation.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff records; proper authority decides; executives accept assignments.
   * **Required inputs:** Performance and portfolio dispositions.
   * **Action:** Record each decision/referral and assignment with one owner, output, acceptance criteria, deadline, dependencies, approver, measure, and review cadence.
   * **Decision point:** Reject shared or charter-inconsistent ownership; route conflicts through COS-006 and reserved decisions through COS-001.
   * **Expected output:** Monthly decision/action plan.
   * **Evidence or record:** Acknowledgments and approval links.
   * **Handoff:** Owners and CEO receive applicable items.
6. **Publish organizational status and synchronize records**
   * **Objective:** Preserve evidence and feed quarterly review.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff; Analytics updates dashboard under its authority.
   * **Required inputs:** Final dispositions and acknowledgments.
   * **Action:** Publish monthly status using the communication standard; update action, decision, initiative, risk, and knowledge records; request dashboard changes; identify accumulated strategic questions for COS-005.
   * **Decision point:** Restrict confidential distribution without omitting the authorized audit record.
   * **Expected output:** Published monthly report, synchronized records, quarterly input queue.
   * **Evidence or record:** Repository, dashboard request, communication log, and COS-005 queue.
   * **Handoff:** COS-003 monitors actions; COS-005 receives trends and strategic questions.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agenda and coordination sequence | Materiality, trend, urgency, risk, horizon | Chief of Staff | None within delegated authority | Monthly agenda |
| KPI definition/method/data quality | Measurement evidence | Director of Analytics | None within measurement authority | Validate/correct dashboard |
| Target interpretation and corrective action | Domain evidence and charter | Functional executive | None unless reserved | Monthly action |
| Strategic priority, material resource/scope, pricing, structure, mission, significant risk | Decision package | CEO | CEO | COS-001 decision |

## Decision Rules

Chief of Staff may coordinate inputs, set agenda/cutoffs, request analysis, recommend sequencing, consolidate recommendations, and delay nonessential work under constrained capacity. CEO decides reserved matters. Analytics owns KPI definitions/methods/reporting consistency; functional executives own objectives, targets, interpretation, corrective action, and outcomes. Escalate persistent material variance, capacity conflict, missing ownership, significant risk, policy conflict, or reserved change.

## Escalation Conditions

| Condition | Escalation Level | Escalation Recipient | Required Information | Execution Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Functional performance correction | Department | Functional executive | Variance, evidence, action, measure/date | Owner proceeds |
| Cross-functional capacity/dependency conflict | Cross-functional | Chief of Staff via COS-006 | Impact, commitments, options | Pause contested change |
| Reserved or significant-risk decision | Executive | CEO via COS-001 | Complete recommendation | Pause affected commitment |
| Measurement-quality failure | Department/cross-functional | Director of Analytics; Chief of Staff coordinates impacts | Defect, affected reports, correction date | Mark evidence unreliable |

## Success Criteria

* All material performance and initiative-health exceptions receive an evidence-backed disposition.
* Corrective actions preserve measurement and functional authority and have one owner and review date.
* Reserved matters enter COS-001 and material initiative changes subsequently enter COS-002.
* The monthly record and quarterly input queue publish within two business days.

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may collect/reconcile reports, detect variances and contradictions, draft exception agendas and summaries, monitor actions, and synchronize records. Humans retain functional interpretation, targets, corrective choices, risk acceptance, and reserved approvals. Integrations may include dashboard, registers, portfolio, repository, calendar, and communications. Automation may not redefine KPIs, infer causation/approval, choose strategy, or overwrite validated functional knowledge.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Quarterly during COS-007.
* **Event-based review triggers:** repeated reporting failure, material measurement/control failure, cadence change, governance change, or automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** Chief of Staff.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Director of Analytics, participating executives, CEO for reserved changes.
