# Chief of Staff Executive Procedure Library

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Chief of Staff  
**Approver:** CEO  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-19

This directory contains the controlled Chief of Staff procedures listed in the [Executive Procedure Catalog](../PROCEDURE-CATALOG.md).

## Procedure Sequence

1. [COS-001: Executive Decision Facilitation](COS-001-executive-decision-facilitation.md) creates the governed decision record.
2. [COS-002: Strategic Initiative Intake](COS-002-strategic-initiative-intake.md) admits an approved initiative to the portfolio.
3. [COS-003: Weekly Executive Review](COS-003-weekly-executive-review.md) monitors execution, dependencies, risks, and current decisions.
4. [COS-004: Monthly Executive Review](COS-004-monthly-executive-review.md) evaluates organizational performance, initiative health, capacity, and corrective action.
5. [COS-005: Quarterly Strategic Review](COS-005-quarterly-strategic-review.md) evaluates direction, portfolio fit, capability, major risks, and learning.

The following control procedures may be triggered at any point without becoming prerequisites for the sequence above:

* [COS-006: Cross-functional Issue Escalation](COS-006-cross-functional-issue-escalation.md) resolves or routes multi-function conflicts.
* [COS-007: Governance Review](COS-007-governance-review.md) validates authority, controls, lifecycle, and implementation traceability.
* [COS-008: Organizational Risk Assessment](COS-008-organizational-risk-assessment.md) integrates and monitors organizational risk.

All decision, initiative, review, escalation, governance, and risk records created by these procedures follow the [Organizational Knowledge Standard](../../standards/organizational-knowledge-standard.md). The Chief of Staff governs repository placement and lifecycle; each functional executive retains substantive ownership of knowledge from that executive's function.
