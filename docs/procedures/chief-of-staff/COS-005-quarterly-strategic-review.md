# COS-005: Quarterly Strategic Review

**Procedure ID:** COS-005  
**Procedure Name:** Quarterly Strategic Review  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner / Accountable Executive:** Chief of Staff  
**Approver:** CEO  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-19  
**Review Frequency:** Quarterly and upon a material governance trigger

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Execute the quarterly strategic governance cycle so objectives, portfolio composition, capability, executive effectiveness, major risks, opportunities, and learning are evaluated as one system. The outcome is CEO-approved direction and a coherent 90+ day portfolio; the impact is reduced strategic drift and stronger long-term customer and organizational value.

## Scope

Applies to the quarterly cadence and extraordinary strategic review. It does not replace annual mission/direction review, weekly/monthly operations, functional strategy work, or CEO decision authority.

## Trigger

**Scheduled trigger:** quarterly strategic review calendar and preparation milestones.  
**Event-driven triggers:** major external change, persistent portfolio failure, significant organizational risk, CEO request, or governance finding requiring strategic review.

## Preconditions

* Monthly evidence, Analytics-validated strategic KPI view, portfolio baseline, risk assessment, and organizational learning synthesis are current.
* Functional executives submit outlooks spanning operational, tactical, and strategic horizons.
* Strategic decision requests identify owner, alternatives, resource/capacity impact, risks, and CEO decision date.
* CEO and required executives or authorized delegates are available.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Build and validate the quarterly strategic evidence base**
   * **Objective:** Create one decision-ready view of organizational performance and context.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff coordinates; source executives validate substance.
   * **Required inputs:** All quarterly inputs.
   * **Action:** Reconcile strategic KPI trends, customer/research evidence, portfolio outcomes, capacity, risk, governance findings, and learning; label facts, assumptions, interpretations, limitations, confidence, and conflicting evidence.
   * **Decision point:** Return material gaps to owners; disclose nonblocking uncertainty; defer unsupported strategic recommendations.
   * **Expected output:** Validated strategic evidence book.
   * **Evidence or record:** Source/validation matrix and unresolved-evidence log.
   * **Handoff:** Accepted evidence to Step 2.
2. **Frame strategic questions and CEO decisions**
   * **Objective:** Convert evidence into explicit choices rather than broad conversation.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff; decision owners supply recommendations.
   * **Required inputs:** Evidence book and proposed decisions.
   * **Action:** For each question state situation, evidence, analysis, recommendation, alternatives, risks, capacity/resource trade-offs, opportunity cost, expected outcomes, confidence, decision owner, implementation owner, and decision deadline.
   * **Decision point:** Route domain-only decisions to functional executives; retain all reserved matters for CEO via COS-001.
   * **Expected output:** Prioritized strategic agenda and decision packages.
   * **Evidence or record:** Authority classification and agenda rationale.
   * **Handoff:** CEO confirms decision agenda.
3. **Evaluate objectives, opportunities, capability, and major risks**
   * **Objective:** Test continued strategic fit across the whole organization.
   * **Responsible:** CEO decides direction; Chief of Staff facilitates; executives own domain inputs.
   * **Required inputs:** Strategic agenda, evidence book, COS-008.
   * **Action:** Compare objectives and opportunities against mission, customer value, evidence, capacity, capability, resources, risk, sustainability, and opportunity cost; record agreement, dissent, uncertainty, and required evidence.
   * **Decision point:** Significant risk, mission alignment, strategic direction, structure, or major capability investment remains with CEO.
   * **Expected output:** CEO direction and validated strategic assumptions.
   * **Evidence or record:** Decision records and assumption register updates.
   * **Handoff:** Direction to portfolio evaluation.
4. **Evaluate and rebalance the initiative portfolio**
   * **Objective:** Align initiatives and capacity to CEO direction.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff coordinates; accountable executives recommend; CEO approves material priorities/resources.
   * **Required inputs:** Portfolio assessment, CEO direction, capacity constraints.
   * **Action:** Classify initiatives as continue, correct, sequence, pause, combine, close, or submit for material change; assess duplication, saturation, strategic fit, customer value, KPI evidence, risks, dependencies, and sunk-cost bias.
   * **Decision point:** Material scope, resource, priority, launch, or risk change uses COS-001; approved new/changed initiatives use COS-002.
   * **Expected output:** Approved portfolio direction and decision queue.
   * **Evidence or record:** Per-initiative rationale, authority, owner, and effective date.
   * **Handoff:** Approved changes to COS-002 and owners.
5. **Assess organizational and executive capability**
   * **Objective:** Identify capability and coordination improvements needed for the strategy.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff coordinates; CEO owns structural/priority decisions.
   * **Required inputs:** Governance findings, review performance, capacity, executive inputs.
   * **Action:** Evaluate coordination, decision quality, executive effectiveness, knowledge flow, process friction, measurement readiness, and automation readiness; assign improvement recommendations to the chartered owner.
   * **Decision point:** Organizational structure, major investment, or strategic priority changes require CEO approval; functional improvements remain with chartered executives.
   * **Expected output:** Capability improvement plan and decision referrals.
   * **Evidence or record:** Finding, evidence, owner, output, measure, date, approval.
   * **Handoff:** COS-007 governs governance actions; owners receive functional actions.
6. **Publish direction and establish the next-quarter baseline**
   * **Objective:** Translate review results into controlled organizational execution.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff; CEO approves direction; Analytics governs dashboard updates.
   * **Required inputs:** Decisions, portfolio direction, capability plan.
   * **Action:** Record CEO decisions; publish strategic summary; update registers, portfolio, risks, knowledge, and review calendar; issue assignments with one owner and acceptance criteria; request dashboard changes; schedule monthly/weekly checkpoints.
   * **Decision point:** No unapproved direction or portfolio change enters the baseline.
   * **Expected output:** Next-quarter baseline, communications, assignments, review schedule.
   * **Evidence or record:** Approval links, acknowledgments, versioned records, distribution log.
   * **Handoff:** COS-003 and COS-004 monitor execution; COS-008 monitors risks.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Review framing and coordination sequence | Materiality, horizon, risk, decision readiness | Chief of Staff | None within delegated authority | Strategic agenda |
| Functional recommendation | Charter-specific evidence | Functional executive | None unless reserved | Include in strategic synthesis |
| KPI definition/method | Measurement evidence | Director of Analytics | None within measurement authority | Validated evidence |
| Direction, priorities, major investments, structure, mission, significant risk, final portfolio decision | Constitutional framework and decision package | CEO | CEO | Direction/approval |

## Decision Rules

Chief of Staff may coordinate, request analysis, synthesize, recommend priorities/sequencing, assign work to the proper executive, and delay nonessential work when capacity is constrained. CEO establishes strategy, priorities, major investments, organizational structure, risk decisions, and final direction. Functional executives retain domain authority; Analytics retains measurement authority. Escalate strategic conflict, material investment/resource trade-off, significant risk, mission concern, structural issue, unresolved executive disagreement, or governance conflict.

## Success Criteria

* CEO direction and every reserved decision are explicit and recorded.
* Every initiative receives a charter-correct disposition and material changes flow through COS-001 then COS-002.
* Major risks, capability gaps, and strategic assumptions have owners and review dates.
* The next-quarter baseline is acknowledged before execution begins.

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may reconcile evidence, detect trends/duplicates/capacity conflicts, draft strategic books and disposition matrices, track decisions, and synchronize records. Humans retain strategic judgment, risk acceptance, portfolio prioritization, functional recommendations, KPI governance, and every CEO-reserved approval. Integrations may include dashboard, portfolio, registers, repository, calendar, and communications. Automation must not choose strategy, infer CEO approval, redefine measures, or convert scenario analysis into commitments.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Quarterly during COS-007 after completion of this review.
* **Event-based review triggers:** strategic decision failure, skipped approval, repeated evidence gap, governance change, material risk, or automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** Chief of Staff.
* **Supporting reviewers:** CEO, Director of Analytics, and all functional executives.
