# COS-008: Organizational Risk Assessment

**Procedure ID:** COS-008  
**Procedure Name:** Organizational Risk Assessment  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner / Accountable Executive:** Chief of Staff  
**Approver:** CEO  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-19  
**Review Frequency:** Quarterly, with weekly monitoring of material risks, and upon a material trigger

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Create and maintain an integrated view of strategic, operational, customer, financial, technical, ethical, governance, capacity, dependency, and organizational risks so each has the correct owner, controls, escalation, and review. The outcome is timely risk treatment and CEO visibility of significant exposure; the impact is greater resilience, trust, and decision quality.

## Scope

Applies to organization-level and cross-functional risk aggregation, periodic assessment, and material emerging risks. It does not transfer functional risk ownership to the Chief of Staff, replace specialist analysis, authorize legal conclusions, set risk appetite, or accept significant risk on behalf of the CEO.

## Trigger

**Scheduled triggers:** weekly material-risk monitoring; monthly performance-risk roll-up; quarterly full assessment; annual organizational health input.  
**Event-driven triggers:** new material risk; control failure; threshold breach; significant incident; initiative/decision/governance review finding; major external change; executive or CEO request.

## Preconditions

* Risk taxonomy, severity/likelihood method, escalation thresholds, and dashboard definitions are validated by the Director of Analytics where measurement applies.
* Each known risk has a proposed functional owner and source evidence.
* Current decision, initiative, issue, governance, KPI, and prior risk records are accessible.
* Immediate safety, ethical, privacy, legal, customer-trust, or continuity threats receive containment before the full assessment.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Collect and reconcile risk signals**
   * **Objective:** Establish a complete, nonduplicate evidence base across functions and records.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff; functional executives validate substantive inputs.
   * **Required inputs:** All required inputs.
   * **Action:** Search and reconcile risk submissions, thresholds, incidents, decisions, initiatives, escalations, governance findings, customer signals, and prior actions; combine duplicates while retaining source ownership and links.
   * **Decision point:** Require immediate containment for imminent/material threats; return unsupported risk claims for evidence without suppressing precautionary escalation.
   * **Expected output:** Consolidated risk candidate set and containment log.
   * **Evidence or record:** Sources, duplicate rationale, owner, timestamp, current controls.
   * **Handoff:** Functional owner acknowledges each candidate.
2. **Validate ownership, evidence, and classification**
   * **Objective:** Put each risk with the correct chartered owner and distinguish fact from uncertainty.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff coordinates; functional owner validates substance; Analytics validates measures.
   * **Required inputs:** Candidate set, charters, evidence, measurement method.
   * **Action:** Write each risk as cause-event-impact; classify category, affected objectives/customers/initiatives, horizon, likelihood/exposure method, velocity, detectability, existing controls, confidence, limitations, and dependencies; name one functional risk owner.
   * **Decision point:** Cross-functional risk keeps one owner with supporters; disputed ownership invokes COS-006; measurement disputes go to Analytics.
   * **Expected output:** Validated risk records.
   * **Evidence or record:** Owner validation, method reference, evidence/assumption labels.
   * **Handoff:** Validated records to prioritization.
3. **Prioritize exposure and control effectiveness**
   * **Objective:** Identify which risks require monitoring, treatment, or immediate executive decision.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff coordinates; risk owner assesses controls; Analytics validates scoring/reporting.
   * **Required inputs:** Validated risks, controls, thresholds, dependencies.
   * **Action:** Apply the approved assessment method; evaluate inherent/current exposure, control design/operation, residual exposure, trajectory, interdependencies, customer trust, mission impact, and risk of inaction; create an organizational risk heat/list view without redefining measures.
   * **Decision point:** Any significant risk, threshold breach, mission/ethical concern, material investment, or cross-organizational exposure is escalated to CEO.
   * **Expected output:** Prioritized risk profile and control gaps.
   * **Evidence or record:** Method, ratings/indicators, rationale, validation, limitations.
   * **Handoff:** Material risks to treatment/decision planning.
4. **Define treatment, contingency, and decision requirements**
   * **Objective:** Turn exposure into accountable, measurable action.
   * **Responsible:** Functional risk owner proposes; Chief of Staff coordinates; CEO decides significant risk.
   * **Required inputs:** Prioritized profile, capacity/resources, applicable authority.
   * **Action:** For each material risk specify avoid, reduce, transfer where legitimately available, monitor, or accept; name actions, one owner, supporters, outputs, control evidence, resources, dates, leading indicators, trigger thresholds, contingency, communications, and residual risk.
   * **Decision point:** Chief of Staff may coordinate and recommend but may not accept significant risk or approve reserved investment/strategy; use COS-001 for CEO decision and COS-002 for an approved new initiative.
   * **Expected output:** Treatment plan and decision queue.
   * **Evidence or record:** Recommendation, alternatives, cost/benefit, residual exposure, approvals.
   * **Handoff:** Owners accept actions; CEO packages go to COS-001.
5. **Publish assessment and synchronize oversight**
   * **Objective:** Give executives an actionable risk view and preserve auditability.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff; Analytics governs dashboard representation.
   * **Required inputs:** Profile, treatments, decisions, communications restrictions.
   * **Action:** Update the risk register; publish an executive assessment stating situation, evidence, analysis, recommendation, risks, decision required, and confidence; request dashboard changes; add weekly/monthly/quarterly checkpoints; communicate to affected owners and CEO.
   * **Decision point:** Restrict sensitive distribution while preserving the authorized record and required escalation.
   * **Expected output:** Risk register, executive risk report, dashboard/cadence updates.
   * **Evidence or record:** Versions, recipients, acknowledgments, decision links.
   * **Handoff:** COS-003 monitors urgent actions; COS-004 reviews trends; COS-005 reviews strategic exposure.
6. **Monitor controls, reassess, and close or escalate**
   * **Objective:** Verify treatment effectiveness and preserve learning.
   * **Responsible:** Risk owner validates controls/outcomes; Chief of Staff monitors lifecycle; Analytics validates measures.
   * **Required inputs:** Treatment/control evidence and indicators.
   * **Action:** At each checkpoint compare indicators and residual exposure to threshold; record improving/stable/worsening, control result, new evidence, action status, and recommended change; close only when criteria and owner validation are met.
   * **Decision point:** Threshold breach, failed control, increasing significant exposure, or missed action triggers immediate reassessment and CEO escalation; systemic governance failure invokes COS-007.
   * **Expected output:** Current risk state, verified closure/escalation, learning asset.
   * **Evidence or record:** Control test, owner validation, closure rationale, lessons.
   * **Handoff:** Learning to repository; procedure/control findings to COS-007.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aggregation, prioritization, review cadence, coordination | Approved method, exposure, urgency | Chief of Staff | None within coordination authority | Register/report/monitor |
| Functional risk treatment within charter/budget/strategy | Domain evidence and approved constraints | Functional executive | None unless reserved | Treatment action |
| Risk measure/method/dashboard definition | Measurement evidence | Director of Analytics | None within measurement authority | Validated risk view |
| Significant risk acceptance/direction or reserved investment/strategy | COS-001 risk package | CEO | CEO | Explicit decision/conditions |

## Decision Rules

Chief of Staff may request assessments, consolidate risks, require reporting/containment, recommend priority and sequencing, coordinate treatments, and delay nonessential work when capacity is constrained. CEO decides significant-risk acceptance and all reserved strategic/material responses. Functional executives own risks and treatments in their domains; Analytics owns risk-measure definitions/methods/reporting consistency. Escalate significant or fast-moving exposure, threshold breach, failed control, ethical/legal/privacy/safety/customer-trust concern, mission issue, material resource requirement, disputed ownership, or governance conflict.

## Escalation Conditions

| Condition | Escalation Level | Escalation Recipient | Required Information | Execution Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Functional risk within authority/threshold | Department | Functional executive | Risk record and treatment | Treat and report |
| Cross-functional ownership/control dependency | Cross-functional | Chief of Staff via COS-006 | Owners, exposures, controls, options | Coordinate; contain disputed exposure |
| Significant/reserved/mission/ethical risk | Executive | CEO via COS-001 | Evidence, exposure, options, residual risk, recommendation | Immediate containment; no acceptance inferred |
| Governance/control-system failure | Executive | COS-007; CEO if significant | Finding, affected artifacts/actions | Pause noncompliant path |

## Success Criteria

* Every material risk has one charter-correct owner, evidence/confidence, controls, treatment, thresholds, and review date.
* Significant risk is promptly contained and explicitly decided by the CEO; acceptance is never inferred.
* Dashboard measures remain Analytics-governed and functional risk ownership remains intact.
* Risk records are deduplicated, synchronized with decisions/initiatives, and closed only with evidence.

## Risks and Controls

| Risk | Impact | Control | Control Owner | Required Evidence | Escalation Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Significant risk accepted implicitly | Unauthorized exposure | Explicit CEO decision gate | Chief of Staff | COS-001 approval | Any missing decision |
| Risk owner ambiguity | Untreated risk | One-owner charter validation | Chief of Staff | Owner acknowledgment | Any disputed material risk |
| Misleading scoring | Wrong priority | Analytics-governed method/limitations | Director of Analytics | Validation record | Material method/data defect |
| Duplicate/stale risk | Conflicting oversight | Source reconciliation and lifecycle checkpoints | Chief of Staff | Duplicate/closure evidence | Material overlap or missed review |
| Sensitive-risk communication failure | Harm or concealment | Access-controlled record plus required recipient log | Chief of Staff | Distribution/audit record | CEO/owner not informed when required |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Incomplete evidence | Risk owner for analysis; CEO for significant decision | Uncertainty explicit; precaution proportionate | Assumptions, confidence, limitations, review date | Evidence plan |
| Executive unavailable | Chief of Staff for coordination | Delegate/temporary containment; no risk-acceptance transfer | Delegation, gap, interim control | Required owner/CEO review |

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may collect/deduplicate signals, detect threshold breaches, map dependencies, draft cause-event-impact statements, calculate approved indicators, monitor controls, and route alerts/updates. Humans retain substantive validation, treatment selection, ethical/contextual judgment, target/threshold ownership where applicable, and all significant-risk/CEO approvals. Integrations may include risk/decision/initiative registers, dashboard, repository, monitoring sources, calendar, and communications. Automation must not infer risk acceptance, create legal conclusions, redefine scoring, suppress precautionary escalation, or close risk without owner-validated evidence.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Weekly material-risk monitoring, monthly roll-up, quarterly full assessment and procedure review.
* **Event-based review triggers:** threshold breach, incident, failed control, significant new evidence, governance change, repeated exception, or automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** Chief of Staff.
* **Supporting reviewers:** all functional risk owners, Director of Analytics, CEO for significant/reserved risk.
