# COS-002: Strategic Initiative Intake

**Procedure ID:** COS-002  
**Procedure Name:** Strategic Initiative Intake  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner / Accountable Executive:** Chief of Staff  
**Approver:** CEO  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-19  
**Review Frequency:** Quarterly and upon a material governance trigger

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

This procedure admits approved strategic work into the organizational initiative portfolio through a single evidence, ownership, capacity, measurement, and governance gate. The outcome is a portfolio containing only authorized, nonduplicate, execution-ready initiatives. The impact is clearer priorities, accountable delivery, and reduced initiative saturation and rework.

## Scope

Applies to proposed work meeting the Strategic Initiative Standard and to approved material changes to an existing initiative. It does not approve strategy, pricing, investments, launches, or functional work; it does not replace COS-001; and it does not admit routine departmental tasks.

## Trigger

**Scheduled triggers:** portfolio intake window preceding COS-004 or COS-005; deferred intake reaches its resubmission date.  
**Event-driven triggers:** COS-001 approves a decision that creates or materially changes an initiative; a chartered executive submits an approved proposal; COS-006 produces an approved cross-functional corrective initiative.

## Preconditions

* A decision record establishes approval from the applicable authority.
* The proposal names one proposed accountable executive and separates supporting executives.
* The submitting executive has validated the substantive accuracy of the proposal.
* No execution commitment has begun beyond explicitly approved discovery or risk-containment work.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Register and deduplicate intake**
   * **Objective:** Create one traceable candidate and prevent parallel initiatives addressing the same objective.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff.
   * **Required inputs:** Approved decision and proposal.
   * **Action:** Assign an intake ID; search the portfolio, decision register, and Knowledge Repository by objective, customer problem, owner, deliverables, and expected outcome; link a material match instead of creating a duplicate.
   * **Decision point:** Merge, amend, return, or continue as a distinct candidate based on overlap and approved scope.
   * **Expected output:** Unique candidate or documented duplicate disposition.
   * **Evidence or record:** Search terms, matches, rationale, and source decision link.
   * **Handoff:** Distinct or amendment candidate proceeds to Step 2.
2. **Verify authority and strategic eligibility**
   * **Objective:** Confirm intake is approved strategic work, not an unapproved decision or routine task.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff; functional owner validates domain classification.
   * **Required inputs:** Candidate, approval record, governance sources.
   * **Action:** Verify scope against the Strategic Initiative Standard; confirm reserved approvals and decision conditions; identify the planning horizon and customer/organizational value.
   * **Decision point:** Return routine work to its functional executive; route missing or ambiguous approval to COS-001; route governance conflict to COS-007.
   * **Expected output:** Eligibility and authority determination.
   * **Evidence or record:** Cited authority, approval, conditions, and disposition.
   * **Handoff:** Eligible candidate proceeds to Step 3.
3. **Validate ownership and contributions**
   * **Objective:** Establish one accountable executive and bounded supporting commitments.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff coordinates; proposed owner and supporters validate commitments.
   * **Required inputs:** Initiative definition, charters, dependency plan.
   * **Action:** Confirm sponsor, one accountable executive, supporting executives, functional deliverables, acceptance criteria, dependency dates, decision checkpoints, and whether implementation ownership was retained or transferred.
   * **Decision point:** A disputed owner or unaccepted dependency invokes COS-006; the Chief of Staff does not become default owner.
   * **Expected output:** Signed responsibility and dependency map.
   * **Evidence or record:** Dated acceptances and any constraints.
   * **Handoff:** Accountable executive accepts total delivery accountability.
4. **Validate readiness, capacity, and measurement**
   * **Objective:** Admit only executable initiatives with trustworthy measures.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff; accountable executive owns plan; Director of Analytics validates measurement.
   * **Required inputs:** Evaluation, plan, current portfolio, KPI plan.
   * **Action:** Verify objectives, deliverables, milestones, success/completion criteria, resources, capacity, risks, dependencies, reporting cadence, review/closure path, and knowledge capture. Obtain Analytics validation of definitions, methods, sources, quality, and dashboard reporting.
   * **Decision point:** Defer when capacity or required inputs are unavailable; escalate material priority/resource conflicts or CEO-reserved change through COS-001.
   * **Expected output:** Readiness assessment and measurement validation.
   * **Evidence or record:** Gate results, deficiencies, owners, and cure dates.
   * **Handoff:** Complete candidate proceeds to Step 5.
5. **Make the intake disposition**
   * **Objective:** Produce a controlled portfolio-admission result.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff for gate disposition; applicable authority for any new business decision.
   * **Required inputs:** Eligibility, ownership, readiness, capacity, and measurement records.
   * **Action:** Mark accepted, conditionally accepted, deferred, returned, or escalated. Conditional acceptance must name each condition, verifier, deadline, and execution limit.
   * **Decision point:** Chief of Staff may sequence accepted work and delay nonessential work under capacity constraints, but may not alter strategic direction, approved scope, functional priorities within a charter, or reserved decisions.
   * **Expected output:** Signed intake disposition.
   * **Evidence or record:** Rationale, conditions, authority, effective date, and next review.
   * **Handoff:** Accepted candidates proceed to Step 6; others receive an explicit resubmission or escalation route.
6. **Create the portfolio baseline and communicate**
   * **Objective:** Establish the authoritative initiative record and operating cadence.
   * **Responsible:** Chief of Staff; accountable executive verifies substantive content.
   * **Required inputs:** Accepted disposition and validated baseline.
   * **Action:** Add or update one portfolio record with lifecycle stage, sponsor, accountable executive, supporters, objective, milestones, measures, risks, dependencies, conditions, horizon, cadence, next checkpoint, and source decision. Publish the validated initiative asset and send assignments.
   * **Decision point:** Do not activate execution until all blocking conditions and owner acknowledgments are complete.
   * **Expected output:** Active portfolio baseline, knowledge asset, assignments, and dashboard handoff.
   * **Evidence or record:** Portfolio version, acknowledgments, repository acceptance, and Analytics dashboard request.
   * **Handoff:** COS-003 monitors weekly execution; COS-004 evaluates monthly health; COS-005 evaluates quarterly portfolio fit.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic eligibility and intake completeness | Standard criteria, approval, gate evidence | Chief of Staff | None within coordination authority | Accept, condition, defer, return, or escalate |
| Initiative business scope, targets, and delivery commitments | Approved direction and domain evidence | Accountable executive | Applicable authority; CEO when reserved | Baseline or COS-001 |
| KPI definition and method | KPI plan and data quality | Director of Analytics | None within measurement authority | Validate or return measurement plan |
| Material portfolio priority/resource conflict | Portfolio evidence and opportunity cost | CEO | CEO | Sequence, pause, or reject |

## Decision Rules

The Chief of Staff may operate the gate, reject duplicates, return incomplete submissions, confirm ownership, set portfolio metadata and cadence, recommend priority/sequencing, and delay nonessential admission when capacity is constrained. CEO approval is required for strategic direction, major investment, pricing, organizational structure, significant risk, mission alignment, major priorities, or material changes involving them. Functional executives own domain plans, commitments, targets, corrective action, and outcomes; Analytics owns measurement definitions and methods. Escalate any missing authority, disputed ownership, material capacity conflict, significant risk, policy conflict, or unresolvable dependency.

## Escalation Conditions

| Condition | Escalation Level | Escalation Recipient | Required Information | Execution Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Routine departmental work | Department | Functional executive | Classification and rationale | Remove from intake |
| Disputed ownership/dependency | Cross-functional | Chief of Staff via COS-006 | Positions, impacts, options, deadline | Pause admission |
| Reserved decision or material priority/resource conflict | Executive | CEO via COS-001 | Portfolio impact, alternatives, recommendation | Pause affected commitment |
| Governance conflict | Executive | COS-007, then CEO if unresolved | Sources and compliant options | Stop conflicting path |

## Success Criteria

* Every admitted initiative has source approval, one accountable executive, accepted dependencies, measures, completion criteria, risks, and cadence.
* No duplicate or unapproved initiative enters the portfolio.
* Blocking conditions are cleared before execution and all assignees acknowledge their commitments.
* Weekly, monthly, and quarterly review handoffs are scheduled without circular intake dependencies.

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may perform field validation, duplicate matching, governance-link checks, capacity summaries, deadline monitoring, baseline drafting, assignment routing, and portfolio/repository/dashboard synchronization. Human approval remains required for reserved decisions, risk acceptance, ownership commitments, target setting, and material scope or priority changes. Potential integrations include the decision register, portfolio, Knowledge Repository, KPI registry, dashboard, calendars, and communications. Automation may not create approval, select strategy, assign itself ownership, redefine KPIs, or bypass a failed gate.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Quarterly during COS-007.
* **Event-based review triggers:** Duplicate admission, ownership failure, skipped approval, repeated conditional intake, material initiative failure, or governance change.
* **Review owner:** Chief of Staff.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Director of Analytics; affected functional executives; CEO for reserved-authority changes.
