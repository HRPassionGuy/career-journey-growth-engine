# REV-003: Revenue Forecast Review

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Accountable Executive:** VP of Revenue

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Produce a current, evidence-bounded revenue outlook that supports decisions without converting uncertain projections into commitments or redefining Analytics-governed measures.

## Trigger

The monthly operating cadence, a quarterly strategic review, a material pipeline/pricing/market change, a forecast variance, or an authorized executive request.

## Preconditions

* The forecast horizon, use, decision date, approved KPI definitions, and current actuals are known.
* Prior forecast versions, assumptions, pipeline state, approved pricing, and material risks are accessible.
* Forecasts are identified as estimates; targets, budgets, and strategic commitments remain separately authorized.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Define review scope and reconcile actuals**
   * **Objective:** Create a comparable forecast baseline.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; Analytics validates actuals.
   * **Required inputs:** Horizon, prior forecast, actuals, approved definitions.
   * **Action:** Record forecast ID, as-of date, horizon, intended use, actual revenue, prior forecast, scope, source systems, cut-off, and any restatement.
   * **Decision point:** Pause publication for a material unreconciled data defect; disclose immaterial limitations.
   * **Expected output:** Reconciled baseline and scope.
   * **Evidence or record:** Data validation, version, sources, limitations.
   * **Handoff:** Baseline to driver review.
2. **Validate drivers and assumptions**
   * **Objective:** Make forecast logic transparent and owner-confirmed.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; each functional owner validates its inputs.
   * **Required inputs:** Pipeline, offers, approved price, campaigns, capacity, seasonality, risks.
   * **Action:** Inventory volume, timing, conversion, AOV, retention/LTV where appropriate, approved promotion/price, capacity, dependencies, seasonality, and external factors; label facts, assumptions, interpretations, confidence, sensitivity, and source owner.
   * **Decision point:** Exclude unapproved pricing, campaigns, investments, launches, or strategic changes from the base case; show them only as conditional scenarios.
   * **Expected output:** Assumption register.
   * **Evidence or record:** Owner confirmations, effective dates, uncertainty ranges.
   * **Handoff:** Validated assumptions to scenario construction.
3. **Construct scenarios and assess risk**
   * **Objective:** Express uncertainty honestly and identify forecast sensitivity.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; Analytics validates calculation method.
   * **Required inputs:** Baseline and assumption register.
   * **Action:** Produce base, downside, and upside scenarios with transparent ranges, probability/confidence language, driver sensitivities, pipeline timing, known risks, opportunity impacts, and no double counting.
   * **Decision point:** Material downside, concentration, or assumption failure invokes REV-004; do not present upside as committed.
   * **Expected output:** Validated scenario forecast.
   * **Evidence or record:** Model version, formulas/method reference, sensitivities, validation.
   * **Handoff:** Forecast to variance and action review.
4. **Explain variance and recommend actions**
   * **Objective:** Translate forecast movement into accountable response.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue.
   * **Required inputs:** Scenarios, prior forecast, actuals, objectives.
   * **Action:** Attribute variance to volume, conversion, price/mix, timing, retention, scope, or data changes; separate controllable from external drivers; recommend Revenue actions, evidence needs, or bounded requests to functional owners.
   * **Decision point:** Pricing uses REV-002; strategic/material/resource/priority decisions use COS-001; accepted strategic initiatives use COS-002; conflicts use COS-006.
   * **Expected output:** Variance bridge and recommendation queue.
   * **Evidence or record:** Cause attribution, confidence, owner, due date.
   * **Handoff:** Owners accept actions; decision packages route correctly.
5. **Publish, monitor, and learn**
   * **Objective:** Preserve a single current outlook and improve forecasting.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; Analytics governs reporting representation.
   * **Required inputs:** Forecast, actions, decisions, review cadence.
   * **Action:** Publish situation, evidence, analysis, recommendation, risks, decision required, and confidence; mark version/as-of date; distribute to authorized recipients; monitor triggers; at close compare forecast with actuals and document bias/error causes and learning.
   * **Decision point:** Restate only through a new version with rationale; never silently overwrite.
   * **Expected output:** Published forecast, action tracking, accuracy review.
   * **Evidence or record:** Repository acceptance, recipients, actions, actual comparison.
   * **Handoff:** COS-004 monthly and COS-005 quarterly; REV-001/004/005 receive learning.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forecast assumptions, scenarios, and Revenue response | Validated evidence, uncertainty, charter authority | VP of Revenue | None within approved constraints | Publish/act |
| Measurement definition/method/data representation | Governed measurement evidence | Director of Analytics | None within measurement authority | Validate or disclose limitation |
| Pricing, strategy, material investment, priority or significant risk response | Decision-ready forecast implication | CEO | CEO | COS-001 |

## Decision Rules

The VP of Revenue owns the forecast and revenue interpretation; Analytics owns metric definitions, calculation methodology, quality, and reporting consistency. Functional executives validate their assumptions. A forecast does not approve a target, budget, price, campaign, launch, investment, or strategy.

## Success Criteria

* Forecasts are versioned, reconciled, scenario-based, and traceable to owner-validated assumptions and Analytics-governed measures.
* Unapproved changes appear only as conditional scenarios and never as base-case commitments.
* Material variance, risks, actions, owners, authority, and confidence are explicit.
* Forecast-to-actual learning improves the next review.

## Risks and Controls

| Risk | Impact | Control | Control Owner | Required Evidence | Escalation Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forecast treated as commitment | Bad decisions | Estimate/target distinction and scenario labels | VP of Revenue | Published forecast | Any material misrepresentation |
| Unapproved assumption in base case | Implied authority | Approval-linked assumption register | VP of Revenue | Decision links | Any reserved unapproved driver |
| False precision/data defect | Misleading outlook | Ranges, limitations, Analytics validation | Director of Analytics | Validation record | Material method/data issue |
| Double counting or stale pipeline | Inflated forecast | Source reconciliation and version controls | VP of Revenue | Reconciliation record | Material duplicate/stale item |
| Silent restatement | Lost auditability | Immutable versions and change rationale | VP of Revenue | Version history | Any overwrite |

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may reconcile source data, apply approved forecast methods, flag stale assumptions, draft scenarios/variance bridges, and monitor triggers. Humans retain assumption judgment, interpretation, action selection, functional validation, and all reserved decisions. Automation must not redefine metrics, fabricate confidence, turn estimates into commitments, insert unapproved changes, or silently restate versions.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Monthly, with quarterly strategic roll-up.
* **Event-based review triggers:** material variance, pipeline/pricing/market change, data defect, governance change, repeated exception, or automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** VP of Revenue.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Director of Analytics; Chief of Staff; input-owning executives; CEO for reserved implications.
