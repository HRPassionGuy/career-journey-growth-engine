# VP Revenue Executive Procedure Library

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** VP of Revenue  
**Approver:** CEO  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-19

This directory contains the controlled VP Revenue procedures listed in the [Executive Procedure Catalog](../PROCEDURE-CATALOG.md).

## Procedure Sequence

1. [REV-001: Revenue Opportunity Evaluation](REV-001-revenue-opportunity-evaluation.md) qualifies and prioritizes revenue opportunities.
2. [REV-002: Pricing Recommendation](REV-002-pricing-recommendation.md) develops CEO-ready recommendations when an opportunity includes pricing.
3. [REV-003: Revenue Forecast Review](REV-003-revenue-forecast-review.md) produces an evidence-bounded revenue outlook.
4. [REV-004: Revenue Risk Review](REV-004-revenue-risk-review.md) identifies and treats revenue-domain exposure.
5. [REV-005: Revenue Pipeline Review](REV-005-revenue-pipeline-review.md) reviews conversion flow, stage health, and next actions.

Each procedure may be triggered independently. References between procedures are handoffs, not prerequisites: REV-001 is the intake and prioritization path; REV-002 is the only pricing recommendation path; REV-003 forecasts; REV-004 governs revenue risk; and REV-005 governs pipeline inspection. CEO-reserved decisions use COS-001, approved strategic initiatives use COS-002, cross-functional conflicts use COS-006, and governance conflicts use COS-007.

All records follow the Organizational Knowledge Standard. The VP of Revenue retains substantive ownership of revenue knowledge; the Chief of Staff governs repository organization and cross-functional consistency; the Director of Analytics governs measurement definitions, methods, data quality, and reporting consistency.
