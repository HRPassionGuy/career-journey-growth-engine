# REV-002: Pricing Recommendation

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Accountable Executive:** VP of Revenue

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Develop evidence-based, customer-trust-preserving pricing recommendations for explicit CEO decision. This procedure governs recommendations only; every pricing change requires CEO approval.

## Trigger

A new offer, material pricing signal, approved opportunity evaluation, portfolio review, performance gap, customer insight, or CEO request requires a pricing recommendation.

## Preconditions

* The offer, customer, value proposition, current price/terms, and decision date are defined.
* No price, discount, payment term, bundle price, or economically equivalent change is represented as approved before CEO approval.
* Relevant evidence and Analytics-governed measures are accessible; sensitive data is appropriately handled.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Register scope and authority**
   * **Objective:** Define exactly what economic term may change and prevent implicit implementation.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue.
   * **Required inputs:** Trigger, offer, current state.
   * **Action:** Assign an ID; specify product/audience, list price, discount, bundle allocation, payment terms, promotion dates, geography/channel, current approvals, decision date, and whether the request duplicates an open recommendation.
   * **Decision point:** Treat any customer-facing price or economically equivalent term change as CEO-reserved.
   * **Expected output:** Scoped pricing record and authority declaration.
   * **Evidence or record:** Baseline terms, sources, owner, duplicate search.
   * **Handoff:** Evidence request to Analytics and relevant executives.
2. **Validate customer value and economic baseline**
   * **Objective:** Establish what value is delivered and what current economics show.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; substantive contributors validate their evidence.
   * **Required inputs:** Customer, offer, financial and performance evidence.
   * **Action:** Document customer outcome, value proposition, segments, current conversion/AOV/LTV/margin/refund evidence, purchasing friction, objections, data quality, confounders, facts, assumptions, and confidence.
   * **Decision point:** Stop or request research where the recommendation would rely on deceptive urgency, unsupported value, unfair treatment, or insufficient material evidence.
   * **Expected output:** Validated value and baseline brief.
   * **Evidence or record:** Analytics validation; customer evidence and limitations.
   * **Handoff:** Baseline to option design.
3. **Develop and compare pricing options**
   * **Objective:** Give the CEO meaningful alternatives rather than a predetermined conclusion.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue.
   * **Required inputs:** Baseline, constraints, customer/market evidence.
   * **Action:** Include status quo and viable alternatives; specify price/terms, eligibility, duration, expected customer value, conversion/revenue/margin ranges, cannibalization, accessibility, trust, operational load, reversibility, dependencies, opportunity cost, and confidence.
   * **Decision point:** Material investment, strategic shift, significant risk, or unresolved dependency is separately flagged; the pricing gate remains mandatory regardless.
   * **Expected output:** Option comparison with scenarios.
   * **Evidence or record:** Assumptions, sensitivity ranges, source links, dissent.
   * **Handoff:** Recommended option to decision packaging.
4. **Design implementation and measurement controls**
   * **Objective:** Ensure an approved change can be implemented, evaluated, and reversed responsibly.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; Analytics validates measures; functional owners validate their work.
   * **Required inputs:** Preferred option and alternatives.
   * **Action:** Define effective dates, customer treatment, communications, checkout/system changes, owner-specific deliverables, hypothesis, success/guardrail measures, baseline, evaluation window, rollback/expiry criteria, monitoring, and review date.
   * **Decision point:** Do not assign creative, campaign, community, research, or automation work without the chartered owner's acceptance.
   * **Expected output:** Controlled implementation and evaluation plan.
   * **Evidence or record:** Owner acceptance, measurement validation, rollback conditions.
   * **Handoff:** Complete package to COS-001.
5. **Obtain and record CEO decision**
   * **Objective:** Secure explicit authority before any pricing commitment.
   * **Responsible:** CEO decides; Chief of Staff facilitates; VP of Revenue presents.
   * **Required inputs:** Executive Communication Standard package.
   * **Action:** Present situation, evidence, analysis, recommendation, alternatives, risks, decision required, confidence, implementation, and evaluation; record approve, reject, defer, or conditional approval with exact terms.
   * **Decision point:** Silence, scheduling, draft copy, system preparation, or prior similar approval never constitutes approval.
   * **Expected output:** Explicit decision and approval record.
   * **Evidence or record:** COS-001-linked CEO decision, terms, conditions, dates.
   * **Handoff:** Only approved terms proceed to owner acceptance.
6. **Implement, verify, evaluate, and close**
   * **Objective:** Confirm faithful implementation and learn from actual outcomes.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue owns outcome; functional owners execute accepted deliverables; Analytics validates evaluation.
   * **Required inputs:** Approved terms and plan.
   * **Action:** Verify customer-facing and system terms before release; monitor guardrails; compare results to baseline/scenarios; record customer effects, limitations, unexpected outcomes, and recommendation to retain, revise, roll back, or end.
   * **Decision point:** Any further pricing change returns to Step 1 and requires a new CEO decision; significant harm/risk triggers containment and REV-004/COS-001.
   * **Expected output:** Verified implementation, outcome review, and closed/reopened record.
   * **Evidence or record:** QA, approval-to-implementation trace, results, lessons.
   * **Handoff:** Forecast and pipeline assumptions updated through REV-003/REV-005.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recommend pricing option | Customer value, evidence, economics, risk, alternatives | VP of Revenue | None to recommend | COS-001 package |
| Approve any pricing change | Complete recommendation and exact terms | CEO | CEO | Implement, reject, defer, or condition |
| Functional implementation method | Approved price and bounded domain requirements | Relevant functional executive | None within charter; no term change | Accepted handoff |
| Retain/revise/rollback price | Outcome evidence and approval conditions | CEO for any changed price; VP may execute pre-approved rollback | CEO as applicable | Close or new recommendation |

## Decision Rules

Revenue owns pricing strategy and recommendations. The CEO approves every pricing change. Analytics governs measures, not pricing strategy. Supporting executives own domain execution. The Chief of Staff facilitates cross-functional and CEO decisions but does not author or approve pricing substance.

## Success Criteria

* Every recommendation compares the status quo and viable alternatives using customer value, evidence, economic ranges, risks, and confidence.
* Every pricing change has explicit, traceable CEO approval for exact terms before implementation.
* Implementation matches approved terms and has owner-accepted controls, measures, guardrails, and rollback criteria.
* Results and customer effects are evaluated and preserved.

## Risks and Controls

| Risk | Impact | Control | Control Owner | Required Evidence | Escalation Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unapproved pricing change | Invalid/customer-facing commitment | Explicit CEO approval gate and term match | VP of Revenue | COS-001 record and QA | Any mismatch/missing approval |
| Manipulative or inequitable pricing | Trust/ethical harm | Customer value, transparency, segment treatment review | VP of Revenue | Review rationale | Any material concern |

| Confounded evaluation | Invalid learning | Baseline, change log, evaluation design | Director of Analytics | Evaluation record | Material concurrent change |

| Recommendation with incomplete evidence | VP of Revenue for submission; CEO decides | Gaps/confidence explicit; no approval inference | Assumptions, risk, evidence plan | Defined review/expiry |

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may assemble evidence, model approved scenarios, detect term mismatches, draft packages, monitor guardrails, and route records. Humans retain value/ethical judgment, option selection, substantive validation, functional acceptance, and CEO approval. Automation must never approve or publish a price, change economic terms, infer approval, redefine measures, or conceal limitations.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Quarterly; each pricing decision reviewed on its approved evaluation date.
* **Event-based review triggers:** unapproved variance, guardrail breach, material evidence change, repeated exception, governance change, or automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** VP of Revenue.
* **Supporting reviewers:** CEO; Chief of Staff; Director of Analytics; affected functional owners.
