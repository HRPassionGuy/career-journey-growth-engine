# REV-004: Revenue Risk Review

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Accountable Executive:** VP of Revenue

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Identify, assess, treat, monitor, and escalate revenue-domain risks while preserving functional ownership and synchronizing material exposure with the organizational risk process.

## Trigger

The monthly revenue cadence, a material threshold breach, forecast/pipeline deterioration, failed revenue control or experiment, customer-trust signal, material dependency change, or executive request.

## Preconditions

* Revenue objectives, current forecast and pipeline, approved thresholds, controls, decisions, and prior risks are accessible.
* Immediate customer, ethical, privacy, legal, safety, or continuity threats receive proportionate containment and required escalation before full review.
* Revenue risks remain owned by the VP of Revenue; risks substantively owned by another function are handed to that executive and only tracked as dependencies.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Collect and reconcile revenue risk signals**
   * **Objective:** Establish a complete, nonduplicate risk candidate set.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; source owners validate inputs.
   * **Required inputs:** All required inputs.
   * **Action:** Search forecast, pipeline, pricing, offer, funnel, experiment, customer, control, incident, decision, initiative, and prior risk records; merge duplicates while preserving sources and ownership.
   * **Decision point:** Contain imminent/material threats immediately; do not delay precautionary escalation for incomplete evidence.
   * **Expected output:** Candidate set and containment log.
   * **Evidence or record:** Sources, duplicate rationale, owner, time, action.
   * **Handoff:** Candidate owners acknowledge.
2. **Validate risk statement, ownership, and evidence**
   * **Objective:** Assign one charter-correct owner and express uncertainty honestly.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue coordinates revenue records; substantive owner validates; Analytics validates measures.
   * **Required inputs:** Candidate set, charters, evidence, approved method.
   * **Action:** State cause-event-impact; classify affected customer/objective, horizon, likelihood/exposure, velocity, detectability, current controls, confidence, limitations, dependencies, and one risk owner.
   * **Decision point:** Hand non-Revenue risk to its functional owner; disputed or cross-functional ownership invokes COS-006; retain only dependency tracking in Revenue.
   * **Expected output:** Validated risk records.
   * **Evidence or record:** Owner acknowledgment, evidence labels, method reference.
   * **Handoff:** Revenue risks to prioritization; organizationally material risks to COS-008.
3. **Assess exposure and control effectiveness**
   * **Objective:** Determine monitoring, treatment, and escalation priority.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; Analytics validates measurement; control owners validate operation.
   * **Required inputs:** Validated risks, controls, thresholds, forecast/pipeline implications.
   * **Action:** Apply the approved method to inherent/current/residual exposure, trajectory, concentration, timing, interdependencies, customer trust, mission impact, control design/operation, and risk of inaction.
   * **Decision point:** Significant exposure, pricing implications, material investment, mission/ethical concern, or threshold breach is escalated to CEO; material organizational exposure is synchronized with COS-008.
   * **Expected output:** Prioritized revenue risk profile and control gaps.
   * **Evidence or record:** Ratings/indicators, rationale, validation, limitations.
   * **Handoff:** Material risks to treatment planning.
4. **Define treatment, contingency, and decision path**
   * **Objective:** Turn exposure into bounded, accountable action.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue for revenue risks; dependency owners for their domain; CEO for reserved choices.
   * **Required inputs:** Risk profile, capacity, authority, existing approvals.
   * **Action:** Select avoid, reduce, monitor, transfer where legitimately available, or recommend accept; define actions, one owner, supporters, controls, outputs, measures, triggers, dates, contingency, communications, and residual risk.
   * **Decision point:** The VP may not accept significant risk or approve pricing, strategy, material investment, or another function's treatment. Use COS-001 for CEO decisions and COS-002 for approved new initiatives.
   * **Expected output:** Treatment/contingency plan and decision queue.
   * **Evidence or record:** Options, cost/benefit, residual risk, approvals, owner acceptance.
   * **Handoff:** COS-008 receives material organizational record; owners receive accepted actions.
5. **Publish, monitor, and close or escalate**
   * **Objective:** Keep risk state current and verify control effectiveness.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; risk/control owners validate results; Analytics validates measures.
   * **Required inputs:** Profile, treatment/control evidence, indicators, decisions.
   * **Action:** Update the revenue risk register; publish executive communication; place risks in weekly/monthly/quarterly cadence as appropriate; compare indicators and residual exposure to thresholds; close only with outcome/control evidence and owner validation.
   * **Decision point:** Failed control, worsening significant exposure, missed action, or threshold breach triggers reassessment and CEO/COS-008 escalation; systemic governance failure invokes COS-007.
   * **Expected output:** Current register, verified closure/escalation, learning.
   * **Evidence or record:** Versions, recipients, control tests, closure rationale.
   * **Handoff:** REV-001/003/005 and COS reviews receive material updates.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue risk priority/treatment within approved authority | Exposure, controls, customer effect, evidence | VP of Revenue | None unless reserved | Treat/monitor |
| Domain risk treatment outside Revenue | Charter-correct risk evidence | Relevant functional executive | Applicable authority | Owner handoff; dependency track |
| Indicator/method/reporting definition | Measurement evidence | Director of Analytics | None within measurement authority | Validated view |
| Significant-risk acceptance/direction or reserved response | Decision-ready package | CEO | CEO | COS-001 decision |

## Decision Rules

The VP of Revenue owns revenue-domain risk and treatment within approved authority. Functional executives own risks in their domains. The Chief of Staff integrates organizational risk through COS-008 and coordinates cross-functional conflicts without taking revenue ownership. Analytics owns risk-measure governance. Only the CEO may accept significant risk or approve reserved responses.

## Escalation Conditions

| Condition | Escalation Level | Escalation Recipient | Required Information | Execution Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue risk within authority/threshold | Department | VP of Revenue | Risk and treatment | Treat and monitor |
| Dependency, ownership, capacity, or control conflict | Cross-functional | Chief of Staff via COS-006/COS-008 | Owners, exposure, options | Contain; pause contested action |
| Significant/reserved/customer-trust/mission risk | Executive | CEO via COS-001; COS-008 informed | Exposure, options, residual risk, recommendation | Immediate containment; no inferred acceptance |
| Governance/control-system failure | Executive | COS-007; CEO if significant | Finding and affected actions | Stop noncompliant path |

## Success Criteria

* Every material revenue risk has one charter-correct owner, evidence/confidence, controls, treatment, threshold, and review date.
* Significant risk is contained and explicitly decided by the CEO; acceptance is never inferred.
* Revenue and organizational risk records are synchronized without duplicating ownership or governance.
* Closure requires owner-validated control/outcome evidence.

## Risks and Controls

| Risk | Impact | Control | Control Owner | Required Evidence | Escalation Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Significant risk accepted implicitly | Unauthorized exposure | Explicit CEO decision gate | VP of Revenue / Chief of Staff | COS-001 decision | Any missing decision |
| Risk ownership duplicated with COS-008/other function | Conflicting action | One substantive owner; linked dependency/integration records | Chief of Staff | Owner acknowledgment/link | Any disputed material risk |

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may collect/deduplicate signals, apply approved indicators, flag thresholds, draft risk statements, monitor controls, and synchronize linked records. Humans retain ownership validation, treatment judgment, customer/ethical judgment, control validation, closure, and CEO decisions. Automation must not suppress escalation, transfer ownership, redefine scoring, infer acceptance, or close risk.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Monthly; material risks monitored weekly; quarterly procedure review.
* **Event-based review triggers:** threshold breach, failed control, incident, significant new evidence, governance change, repeated exception, or automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** VP of Revenue.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Chief of Staff; Director of Analytics; affected risk/control owners; CEO for significant risk.
