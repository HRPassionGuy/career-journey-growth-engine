# REV-001: Revenue Opportunity Evaluation

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Accountable Executive:** VP of Revenue

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Evaluate and prioritize revenue opportunities that improve qualified conversion, customer value, purchasing ease, average order value, lifetime value, scalability, and total revenue without sacrificing trust.

## Trigger

A material revenue signal, proposed offer/funnel improvement, planning-cycle request, performance gap, customer insight, or completed experiment requires evaluation.

## Preconditions

* The opportunity is within Revenue authority or has a named functional owner for any non-Revenue execution.
* Available evidence, current priorities, constraints, and prior related recommendations are accessible.
* Immediate ethical, legal, privacy, customer-trust, or significant-risk concerns are contained and escalated before commitment.

| Input | Source or Owner | Quality or Approval Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Opportunity statement and customer problem | VP of Revenue / Research / Community | Specific customer, constraint, mechanism, and horizon |
| Sales, store, email, offer, and funnel evidence | VP of Revenue | Current, source-linked, with limitations |
| KPI and experiment evidence | Director of Analytics | Governed definitions, method, quality, confidence |
| Customer and market insight | Community / Research & Product Development | Pattern validated; facts separated from assumptions |
| Capacity, dependencies, and priorities | Functional executives / Chief of Staff | Owner-confirmed and current |

| RACI role | Participant | Responsibility | Required Input | Expected Output or Handoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Responsible / Accountable | VP of Revenue | Frame, evaluate, rank, recommend, and own revenue outcome | Required inputs | Opportunity record and recommendation |

## Procedure Steps

1. **Register and frame the opportunity**
   * **Objective:** Create a unique, decision-useful opportunity record.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue.
   * **Required inputs:** Trigger, customer problem, source evidence.
   * **Action:** Search for duplicates; state customer, revenue constraint, proposed mechanism, horizon, facts, observations, assumptions, expected value, and confidence.
   * **Decision point:** Return non-Revenue requests to the chartered owner; retain Revenue recommendation ownership only for revenue substance.
   * **Expected output:** Unique opportunity record.
   * **Evidence or record:** Sources, owner, date, duplicate links, evidence labels.
   * **Handoff:** Analytics receives material measurement questions.
2. **Validate evidence and baseline**
   * **Objective:** Establish a credible current-state comparison.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; Director of Analytics validates measures.
   * **Required inputs:** Performance data, definitions, qualitative evidence.
   * **Action:** Document baseline, segment, time window, funnel location, data quality, customer evidence, alternative explanations, limitations, and confidence.
   * **Decision point:** If material evidence is insufficient, define a bounded evidence-gathering test rather than claim impact.
   * **Expected output:** Validated evidence brief.
   * **Evidence or record:** Analytics validation and unresolved gaps.
   * **Handoff:** Validated record to evaluation.
3. **Evaluate value, fit, and feasibility**
   * **Objective:** Compare the opportunity against constitutional and revenue priorities.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; functional executives validate dependencies.
   * **Required inputs:** Evidence brief, capacity, priorities, risks.
   * **Action:** Assess customer value, qualified conversion, friction, AOV/LTV, revenue range, scalability, trust, strategic fit, effort, opportunity cost, reversibility, dependencies, and learning value.
   * **Decision point:** Any pricing component invokes REV-002; strategic direction, material investment, significant risk, or priority conflict requires CEO routing through COS-001.
   * **Expected output:** Comparable evaluation and alternatives.
   * **Evidence or record:** Criteria, ranges, assumptions, confidence, dissent.
   * **Handoff:** Candidate to prioritization.
4. **Select disposition and execution path**
   * **Objective:** Produce one accountable next step without overcommitting resources.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue.
   * **Required inputs:** Evaluation and current opportunity queue.
   * **Action:** Rank against active opportunities and select pursue, test, hold, reject, or request evidence; identify one primary revenue experiment where testing is appropriate, owner, outputs, measures, guardrails, dates, and dependencies.
   * **Decision point:** Approved strategic initiatives use COS-002; cross-functional conflicts use COS-006. Do not start execution before required approval and owner acceptance.
   * **Expected output:** Prioritized recommendation and route.
   * **Evidence or record:** Rationale, opportunity cost, approval requirement, review date.
   * **Handoff:** Correct functional owners accept bounded requests.
5. **Communicate, monitor, and learn**
   * **Objective:** Preserve accountability and update the opportunity portfolio using outcomes.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; Analytics validates results.
   * **Required inputs:** Accepted route, execution evidence, measures.
   * **Action:** Publish the executive communication; monitor at the appropriate cadence; compare outcomes with baseline and hypothesis; record unexpected effects and recommend scale, revise, stop, or re-evaluate.
   * **Decision point:** Scaling that triggers reserved authority returns to COS-001; material risk invokes REV-004.
   * **Expected output:** Current opportunity status and learning record.
   * **Evidence or record:** Decision, owner acceptance, results, lessons.
   * **Handoff:** Reviews receive forecast, risk, and pipeline impacts as applicable.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prioritize, hold, reject, or request evidence | Customer value, revenue potential, evidence, capacity, risk | VP of Revenue | None within charter and approved constraints | Queue or close |
| Run bounded revenue experiment | Hypothesis, measures, guardrails, capacity | VP of Revenue | Applicable authority if resources/strategy reserved | Functional execution handoff |
| Change pricing, strategy, major investment, priority, or significant risk | Decision-ready package | CEO | CEO | COS-001 decision |

## Decision Rules

The VP of Revenue owns revenue evaluation, ranking, recommendation, and revenue experiments within approved authority. Supporting executives own their domain outputs; Analytics owns measurement governance; the Chief of Staff coordinates cross-functional matters. Evaluation never constitutes approval of pricing, material investment, strategy, or significant risk.

## Escalation Conditions

| Condition | Escalation Level | Escalation Recipient | Required Information | Execution Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue choice within authority | Department | VP of Revenue | Evaluation and constraints | Decide and record |

| Pricing, strategic direction, material investment, priority conflict, significant/mission risk | Executive | CEO via COS-001 | Evidence, alternatives, recommendation, risk | No commitment before decision |
| Governance conflict | Executive | COS-007; CEO if unresolved | Conflicting sources and compliant options | Stop conflicting path |

## Success Criteria

* Every evaluated opportunity has a unique record, evidence/confidence, explicit disposition, one owner, authority route, and review date.
* Prioritization considers customer value and opportunity cost before projected revenue.
* No pricing or reserved commitment begins without explicit CEO approval.
* Outcomes update the opportunity queue and reusable knowledge.

## Risks and Controls

| Risk | Impact | Control | Control Owner | Required Evidence | Escalation Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue-first bias harms trust | Customer/mission harm | Customer value and trust gate | VP of Revenue | Evaluation rationale | Any material concern |
| Optimistic or selective evidence | Misallocation | Evidence labels, alternatives, Analytics validation | VP of Revenue | Validation record | Material limitation omitted |
| Duplicate or conflicting work | Waste | Register search and one-owner rule | VP of Revenue | Search/owner record | Probable duplicate/conflict |
| Unauthorized cross-functional commitment | Ownership conflict | Bounded request and acceptance | Chief of Staff | Handoff record | Unaccepted commitment |
| Too many simultaneous experiments | Confounded learning | One primary revenue experiment | VP of Revenue | Experiment register | More than one primary experiment |

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may collect and deduplicate signals, classify evidence, calculate approved indicators, draft comparisons, monitor dates, and route bounded requests. Humans retain opportunity selection, customer/trust judgment, substantive validation, functional acceptance, experiment decisions, and every reserved approval. Automation must not invent evidence, redefine measures, approve pricing, select strategy, commit another function, or scale an experiment.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Quarterly; opportunity queue monthly.
* **Event-based review triggers:** authority error, material outcome gap, repeated exception, control failure, governance change, or automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** VP of Revenue.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Chief of Staff for consistency; Director of Analytics for measurement; affected executives for handoffs; CEO for reserved authority.
