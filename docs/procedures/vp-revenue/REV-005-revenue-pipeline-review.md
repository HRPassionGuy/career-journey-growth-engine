# REV-005: Revenue Pipeline Review

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Accountable Executive:** VP of Revenue

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Review the end-to-end revenue pipeline to identify the largest qualified-conversion constraint, maintain truthful stage state, prioritize one high-impact response, and improve purchasing ease and customer value.

## Trigger

The weekly revenue cadence, a material stage/conversion threshold breach, a launch or promotion checkpoint, a forecast variance, an executive request, or a significant customer/funnel signal.

## Preconditions

* Pipeline stages, entry/exit criteria, ownership, governed measures, active offers, and review window are defined.
* Source data and prior actions are accessible; material data limitations are known.
* The review does not authorize pricing, campaigns, creative production, community management, research, or automation outside the relevant executive's authority.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Reconcile pipeline scope and state**
   * **Objective:** Establish one current and trustworthy pipeline view.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; Analytics validates measures.
   * **Required inputs:** Stage criteria, source data, prior snapshot.
   * **Action:** Set as-of date/window/segments; reconcile stage entries/exits, duplicates, stale items, value, aging, source, and approved definitions; record changes to stage logic separately for governance review.
   * **Decision point:** Pause material conclusions if data/definition defects make the view misleading; disclose bounded limitations otherwise.
   * **Expected output:** Reconciled pipeline snapshot.
   * **Evidence or record:** Source reconciliation, definitions, quality, exceptions.
   * **Handoff:** Snapshot to performance review.
2. **Evaluate stage performance and customer friction**
   * **Objective:** Identify where qualified attention fails to become customer value and revenue.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; functional owners validate their evidence.
   * **Required inputs:** Snapshot, funnel/offer evidence, objections, campaign context.
   * **Action:** Compare stage volume, conversion, velocity, aging, drop-off, value/mix, cohort/segment, purchasing friction, recurring objections, customer experience, approved thresholds, and prior periods; distinguish fact, interpretation, assumption, and attribution limitations.
   * **Decision point:** Hand channel performance to Marketing, creative quality to Creative, community/customer signal work to Community, research to RPD, and build work to Automation; Revenue retains funnel/conversion interpretation and outcome.
   * **Expected output:** Stage health and constraint analysis.
   * **Evidence or record:** Trends, thresholds, customer evidence, validation, confidence.
   * **Handoff:** Constraint candidates to prioritization.
3. **Select the largest actionable revenue constraint**
   * **Objective:** Concentrate effort and learning on the highest-value constraint.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue.
   * **Required inputs:** Stage analysis, active opportunities, risks, capacity.
   * **Action:** Compare customer value, qualified-conversion impact, friction, revenue range, evidence strength, effort, risk, scalability, learning value, dependencies, and opportunity cost; select one primary revenue constraint and explain deferred candidates.
   * **Decision point:** Pricing routes to REV-002; material risk to REV-004; broader evaluation to REV-001; do not create circular prerequisites—each receiving procedure independently validates its input.
   * **Expected output:** Prioritized constraint and disposition.
   * **Evidence or record:** Criteria, rationale, confidence, deferred queue.
   * **Handoff:** Selected response to action design.
4. **Define and accept actions or experiment**
   * **Objective:** Create bounded, measurable work with one owner per action.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue owns Revenue response; functional executives own their deliverables.
   * **Required inputs:** Selected constraint and authority map.
   * **Action:** Define hypothesis/action, customer outcome, owner, supporters, deliverables, baseline, Analytics-governed success/guardrail measures, dates, dependencies, approval needs, stop/scale criteria, and next checkpoint. Maintain one primary revenue experiment at a time.
   * **Decision point:** Pricing, strategy, major investment, priority, or significant risk uses COS-001; approved strategic initiatives use COS-002; owner/capacity conflicts use COS-006.
   * **Expected output:** Accepted action/experiment plan.
   * **Evidence or record:** Owner acceptance, measurement validation, approval links.
   * **Handoff:** Each functional owner receives only its bounded output request.
5. **Review results, update connected records, and close**
   * **Objective:** Verify action effectiveness and keep pipeline, forecast, risk, and knowledge aligned.
   * **Responsible:** VP of Revenue; Analytics validates results; functional owners validate outputs.
   * **Required inputs:** Action evidence, next pipeline snapshot, approved measures.
   * **Action:** Compare result to baseline/hypothesis and guardrails; record customer impact, confounders, unexpected outcomes, action status, and recommendation; update REV-001 opportunity, REV-003 forecast, and REV-004 risk records only where materially affected.
   * **Decision point:** Scale/revise/stop within authority; route reserved changes correctly; close only when action acceptance and outcome evidence are complete.
   * **Expected output:** Current pipeline, verified action outcome, learning record.
   * **Evidence or record:** Snapshot comparison, validation, decisions, lessons.
   * **Handoff:** COS-003/004 receive material progress; COS-005 receives strategic trend.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Select pipeline constraint and Revenue action | Stage evidence, customer value, capacity, risk | VP of Revenue | None within approved authority | Action/test |
| Define/represent pipeline measures | Measurement requirements and source quality | Director of Analytics | None within measurement authority | Validated pipeline view |
| Execute supporting functional work | Accepted bounded request within charter | Relevant functional executive | Applicable authority | Functional handoff |
| Pricing, strategy, material investment/priority, significant risk | Decision-ready package | CEO | CEO | COS-001 |

## Decision Rules

The VP of Revenue owns pipeline business logic, interpretation, prioritization, conversion actions, and outcomes. Analytics governs definitions, calculation, data quality, and reporting consistency. Supporting executives retain domain execution. Pipeline review does not approve pricing or create shared accountability.

| Condition | Escalation Level | Escalation Recipient | Required Information | Execution Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Success Criteria

* Pipeline state is current, deduplicated, stage-defined, and measurement-validated.
* One primary constraint and one accountable owner per action are explicit; deferred work is documented.
* Supporting work is accepted by chartered owners and pricing/reserved matters receive CEO approval.
* Outcomes update pipeline, forecast, risk, opportunity, and knowledge records without duplication.

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may reconcile records, flag stale/duplicate stages and threshold breaches, apply approved metrics, summarize objections, draft reviews, and monitor actions. Humans retain stage-business judgment, customer/context interpretation, constraint selection, functional acceptance, experiment decisions, and reserved approvals. Automation must not redefine stages/measures, overclaim attribution, change price, assign another function, or infer approval.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Weekly pipeline review; monthly procedure effectiveness review; quarterly strategic trend review.
* **Event-based review triggers:** threshold breach, launch/promotion checkpoint, material forecast/risk change, data defect, repeated exception, governance change, or automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** VP of Revenue.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Director of Analytics; Chief of Staff; affected functional executives; CEO for reserved matters.
