# MKT-005: Marketing Experiment Review

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Accountable Executive:** Marketing Director

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Evaluate a marketing-owned experiment objectively, decide its Marketing disposition, and convert validated results into organizational learning without transferring measurement authority from Analytics.

## Trigger

A marketing experiment reaches its planned checkpoint, stop condition, completion date, guardrail breach, or receives an authorized early-review request.

## Preconditions

* A registered hypothesis identifies the Marketing variable, audience, scope, primary/secondary measures, threshold, duration, guardrails, owner, and approval.
* Baseline and measurement methods were validated by Analytics before execution or limitations are explicitly recorded.
* Offer, pricing, funnel, creative, and community variables remain owned by their respective executives.

|---|---|---|

| Results, baseline, method, quality, sample/duration limits | Director of Analytics | Analytics-validated |
| Revenue, creative, and community context | Respective functional owners | Bounded interpretation; confounders disclosed |
| Customer/trust incidents and guardrail results | Marketing / relevant owners | Complete and timely |

## Procedure Steps

1. **Verify experiment integrity**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director; Analytics validates measurement integrity.
   * **Action:** Confirm original hypothesis, owner, variable, approvals, audience, dates, exposure, planned measures, threshold, guardrails, interventions, deviations, early stops, and concurrent changes.
   * **Decision point:** Classify as evaluable, directionally evaluable, or invalid; never retrofit success criteria after seeing results.
   * **Evidence or record:** Integrity and deviation record.
   * **Handoff:** Evaluable evidence to Analytics.
2. **Produce objective evaluation**
   * **Responsible:** Director of Analytics.
   * **Action:** Compare results with baseline/control and thresholds; report primary and secondary measures, effect size where appropriate, uncertainty, duration/sample limits, data quality, segments, guardrails, unexpected outcomes, alternative explanations, and correlation/causation limits.
   * **Decision point:** Conflicting or insufficient evidence must be labeled, not forced into a win/loss claim.
   * **Evidence or record:** Analytics-validated experiment evaluation.
   * **Handoff:** Evaluation to functional interpretation.
3. **Obtain domain interpretation**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director coordinates; functional executives own their conclusions.
   * **Action:** Separate channel/distribution learning from offer/funnel, creative, and community effects; record favorable and unfavorable findings, dissent, customer impact, and confidence.
   * **Decision point:** Route proposed non-Marketing changes to the proper owner; the review cannot authorize them.
   * **Evidence or record:** Interpretation matrix and owner statements.
   * **Handoff:** Decision-ready record to Marketing.
4. **Choose experiment disposition**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director within authority.
   * **Action:** Decide adopt within existing scope, iterate with a new pre-registered hypothesis, repeat for evidence, stop/revert, or recommend scale. State rationale, scope, target, guardrails, owner, and review date.
   * **Decision point:** Scale that changes strategy, major investment, organizational priority, significant risk, or pricing requires CEO decision via COS-001. Revenue/Creative/Community changes require owner acceptance and authority.
   * **Evidence or record:** Signed disposition and required approvals.
   * **Handoff:** MKT-001/MKT-004 or applicable owner receives bounded action.
5. **Capture and publish organizational learning**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director owns marketing substance; Analytics validates measurement content; Chief of Staff governs repository lifecycle.
   * **Action:** Record hypothesis, measures, thresholds, duration, results, limitations, decision, unexpected outcomes, reusable lesson, rejected interpretations, and next application; link the experiment register, campaigns, channels, KPI registry, and decisions.
   * **Decision point:** Knowledge is not marked validated until substantive and measurement owners complete review.
   * **Evidence or record:** Accepted learning asset and updated experiment register.
   * **Handoff:** Future planning and executive reviews consume the record.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evaluation validity | Method, quality, deviations, limitations | Director of Analytics | None within measurement authority | Valid/directional/invalid |
| Adopt, iterate, repeat, stop, recommend scale | Validated result, customer value, practical impact, risk | Marketing Director | None within approved Marketing scope | Action or recommendation |
| Change another function's work | Domain evidence | Relevant functional executive | Applicable authority | Accepted handoff |
| Material scale/reserved change | Decision-ready package | CEO | CEO | COS-001 |

## Decision Rules

Marketing owns marketing experiments and their business disposition; Analytics owns evaluation definitions and methods. Experiments reduce uncertainty and cannot be used to justify a predetermined answer. Only one primary marketing experiment may affect the same decision context at a time unless Analytics validates separability and Marketing documents capacity and confounder controls.

## Success Criteria

* Every experiment has a pre-existing hypothesis, criteria, guardrails, and traceable disposition.
* Analytics validates every material evaluation and campaign results feed governed KPIs.
* Organizational knowledge captures favorable and unfavorable outcomes, limitations, and reusable lessons.
* No experiment review assigns Marketing revenue, creative, community, or measurement authority.

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may validate fields, freeze versions, assemble results, flag deviations, calculate approved methods, draft comparisons, and route learning. Humans retain hypotheses, practical interpretation, disposition, risk judgment, functional acceptance, and reserved approval. Automation may not alter criteria after results, claim causation, redefine metrics, scale a test, or approve its source procedure.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Quarterly; each experiment at its registered checkpoint.
* **Event-based review triggers:** guardrail breach, invalid evaluation, repeated inconclusive result, knowledge failure, governance change, automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** Marketing Director.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Director of Analytics, Chief of Staff, affected functional executives, CEO for reserved changes.
