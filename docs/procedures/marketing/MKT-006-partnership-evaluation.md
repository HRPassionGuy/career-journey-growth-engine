# MKT-006: Partnership Evaluation

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Accountable Executive:** Marketing Director

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Evaluate and govern marketing, referral, audience, co-marketing, and distribution partnerships for customer value, strategic/channel fit, trust, feasibility, measurable benefit, and risk while retaining partnership ownership in Marketing.

## Trigger

An inbound partnership proposal, identified partner opportunity, referral/channel signal, renewal checkpoint, performance concern, or executive request.

## Preconditions

* The proposed relationship has a named counterparty, customer/audience value, partnership type, scope, and decision deadline.
* Marketing is the accountable owner; contract, legal, pricing, offer, creative, community, analytics, and automation decisions remain with their proper authorities.
* No commitment or public representation has been made before required approval.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Register, classify, and screen**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director.
   * **Action:** Assign an ID; deduplicate; classify partnership type, audience, customer value, objectives, desired action, time horizon, exclusivity, expected obligations, data use, reputation, conflicts, and urgency. Separate facts, assumptions, and claims.
   * **Decision point:** Return vendor procurement, product, employment, pure sales, or other non-Marketing relationships to the correct owner while retaining Marketing ownership only when the core relationship is a marketing/referral/distribution partnership.
   * **Evidence or record:** Intake and classification record.
   * **Handoff:** Qualified opportunity to diligence.
2. **Conduct fit and trust diligence**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director; contributors validate domain evidence.
   * **Action:** Evaluate mission/customer alignment, audience overlap and quality, educational value, brand/reputation, mutual value, channel durability, prior performance, conflicts, privacy/data handling, claims, accessibility, operational burden, exit/reversibility, alternatives, and opportunity cost.
   * **Decision point:** Reject deceptive, exploitative, discriminatory, privacy-unsafe, mission-conflicting, or materially untrustworthy relationships; escalate significant ethical/legal/reputation uncertainty.
   * **Evidence or record:** Diligence file, sources, limitations, confidence.
   * **Handoff:** Viable opportunity to terms and feasibility.
3. **Validate terms, ownership, and feasibility**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director coordinates; each executive owns domain substance.
   * **Action:** Define deliverables, channel rights, schedule, audience/data access, attribution, review/approval rights, exclusivity, use of name/assets, service levels, termination, costs/resources, revenue/commercial effects, creative needs, community role, measurement, and owner acceptance.
   * **Decision point:** Marketing may not agree pricing/offer/funnel terms, create creative assets, commit community operations, define KPIs, build automation, or provide legal approval. Contract/legal review must be obtained from an authorized legal/CEO route where required; this procedure creates no legal executive role.
   * **Evidence or record:** Responsibility/terms matrix and functional acceptances.
   * **Handoff:** Complete evaluation to recommendation.
4. **Evaluate value, risk, and measurement**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director; Analytics validates measures.
   * **Action:** Compare proceed, pilot, renegotiate, hold, reject, and alternatives using customer value, audience fit, qualified reach/leads, referral potential, brand authority, revenue contribution supplied by Revenue, cost/capacity, risk, learning value, confidence, guardrails, targets, baseline, reporting, and exit criteria.
   * **Decision point:** Prefer a bounded pilot when material uncertainty is testable. Material investment, strategic direction, major priority, significant risk, mission issue, or pricing goes to CEO via COS-001.
   * **Evidence or record:** Comparable evaluation, recommendation, Analytics validation.
   * **Handoff:** Decision-ready record to authority.
5. **Decide, formalize, monitor, and learn**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director within authority; CEO for reserved matters.
   * **Action:** Decide pursue, pilot, renegotiate, hold, reject, renew, modify, or exit. Before activation, record approvals, agreement/authorization, one Marketing relationship owner, deliverables, functional owners, measures, communications, escalation contacts, review dates, termination conditions, and repository record. Monitor performance and capture lessons.
   * **Decision point:** Marketing retains partnership accountability even when other executives deliver bounded contributions. Strategic partnerships meeting initiative criteria enter COS-002 after approval; conflicts use COS-006.
   * **Evidence or record:** Decision, agreement reference, operating baseline, reviews, lessons.
   * **Handoff:** MKT-001 for campaigns, MKT-004 for channel performance, MKT-005 for pilots/experiments, and functional owners for accepted work.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Screen/reject/hold/renegotiate partnership | Fit, trust, evidence, feasibility | Marketing Director | None within authority | Close or continue |
| Pursue/pilot/renew/exit within approved constraints | Evaluation, accepted dependencies, measures, risk | Marketing Director | None within delegated authority | Formalize/operate/close |
| Approve revenue, creative, community, measurement inputs | Domain evidence | Respective functional executive | Applicable authority | Accepted contribution |
| Pricing, strategy, major investment/priority, significant risk/mission issue | Decision-ready package | CEO | CEO | COS-001 |

## Decision Rules

Marketing is the sole accountable executive for marketing/referral/distribution partnership evaluation and ongoing relationship strategy. Supporting work never transfers partnership ownership. Marketing may decline an opportunity within its authority and may approve bounded, reversible relationships within approved strategy/resources, but cannot execute contractual authority it has not been granted or approve reserved decisions.

## Success Criteria

* Every partnership has one accountable Marketing owner, verified authority, diligence, accepted functional contributions, measures, risk controls, and exit terms.
* Partnership ownership remains with Marketing through evaluation and operation.
* No pricing, revenue, creative, community, analytics, automation, legal, or reserved authority is assigned to Marketing.
* Partnership outcomes feed Analytics-governed KPIs and reusable organizational knowledge.

## Risks and Controls

| Risk | Impact | Control | Control Owner | Required Evidence | Escalation Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Partner harms trust/mission | Customer/brand harm | Diligence, claims/brand review, exit right | Marketing Director | Diligence and review record | Material concern |
| Unauthorized commitment | Legal/financial/governance harm | Authority and agreement gate | Marketing Director / CEO | Approval/agreement reference | Any missing authority |
| Unclear ownership | Delivery failure | One Marketing owner plus RACI acceptances | Chief of Staff | Responsibility matrix | Disputed/absent owner |
| Misleading value/attribution | Bad renewal/investment | Analytics plan and limitations | Director of Analytics | Validation record | Material quality gap |
| Data/privacy misuse | Trust/legal harm | Data minimization, consent, access and escalation controls | Marketing Director / authorized authority | Data-use record | Any credible breach |

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may deduplicate prospects, collect public evidence, screen required fields, compare approved criteria, monitor obligations/reviews, and draft records. Humans retain partner selection, trust/ethical judgment, relationship commitment, negotiation, functional acceptance, risk acceptance, and reserved approval. Automation may not contact or commit a partner, sign terms, approve pricing/spend, redefine KPIs, create assets, or disclose protected data.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Quarterly for active partnerships and before renewal; procedure annually.
* **Event-based review triggers:** breach, incident, material underperformance, ownership/terms change, repeated exception, governance change, automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** Marketing Director.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Chief of Staff, Director of Analytics, VP Revenue, Creative Director, Director of Community, CEO for reserved changes.
