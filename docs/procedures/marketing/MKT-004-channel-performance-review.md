# MKT-004: Channel Performance Review

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Accountable Executive:** Marketing Director

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Evaluate whether each marketing channel reaches the intended audience efficiently and durably, then decide within Marketing authority to continue, adjust, test, reduce, pause, or recommend investment.

## Trigger

The monthly review cadence, a campaign checkpoint, a material channel signal, a threshold breach, or an executive request.

## Preconditions

* The channel, objective, audience, comparison window, and decision deadline are defined.
* Analytics-governed definitions and current data-quality status are available.
* Revenue, Creative, and Community outcomes are distinguishable from Marketing channel decisions.

## Required Inputs

| Input | Source or Owner | Quality or Approval Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Channel plan, spend/resource use, cadence, campaign tags | Marketing Director | Current and source-linked |
| Reach, engagement, leads, acquisition, conversion-assist data | Director of Analytics | Governed definitions, segmentation, limitations |
| Lead quality and downstream conversion evidence | VP Revenue | Revenue interpretation clearly labeled |
| Asset/content performance context | Creative Director | Creative-owned interpretation and constraints |
| Community/referral signals | Director of Community | Patterns, not isolated incidents |

## Participants and Responsibilities

| RACI role | Participant | Responsibility | Required Input | Expected Output or Handoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Responsible / Accountable | Marketing Director | Own channel interpretation, mix, cadence, corrective action, and outcome | Review inputs | Channel decision record |
| Responsible for measurement | Director of Analytics | Own definitions, methodology, quality, comparisons, and reporting consistency | Data request | Validated performance view |
| Consulted | VP Revenue | Interpret lead quality, conversion path, and revenue effects | Channel cohorts | Revenue findings/recommendation |

## Procedure Steps

1. **Set scope and comparison basis**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director.
   * **Action:** Identify channel, audience, objective, funnel role, period, baseline, prior decisions, resource use, and decision required; deduplicate overlapping reviews.
   * **Decision point:** Separate campaign, content, revenue, and community questions and route domain analysis without transferring accountability.
   * **Evidence or record:** Review charter and source list.
   * **Handoff:** Analytics receives scoped request.
2. **Validate the performance view**
   * **Responsible:** Director of Analytics.
   * **Action:** Apply governed definitions; validate data, segments, time windows, attribution, comparability, leading/lagging measures, anomalies, and limitations. Distinguish facts, observations, interpretations, and assumptions.
   * **Decision point:** Material data weakness produces an evidence plan or bounded decision; it must not produce false precision.
   * **Evidence or record:** Validated scorecard and quality note.
   * **Handoff:** Scorecard to functional interpretation.
3. **Collect bounded functional interpretation**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director coordinates; executives own their findings.
   * **Action:** Obtain Revenue interpretation of lead/conversion quality, Creative interpretation of asset effects, and Community interpretation where engagement/advocacy is relevant. Test alternative explanations and disclose disagreement.
   * **Decision point:** Do not attribute channel outcomes solely to Marketing where evidence shows material offer, creative, funnel, community, seasonality, or measurement effects.
   * **Evidence or record:** Attributed findings, confidence, dissent.
   * **Handoff:** Evidence package to decision.
4. **Decide channel disposition**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director.
   * **Action:** Select continue, adjust audience/cadence/sequence/mix, run MKT-005 experiment, reduce, pause, or recommend expansion. Assess customer value, qualified reach, lead quality, durable value, efficiency, acquisition cost where measurable, opportunity cost, risk, and confidence.
   * **Decision point:** Marketing may change channels and distribution within constraints; offer/funnel changes go to Revenue, asset changes to Creative, community changes to Community, KPI changes to Analytics, and material investment/priority changes to CEO via COS-001.
   * **Evidence or record:** Decision, rationale, owner, target, review date.
   * **Handoff:** Accepted corrective actions to owners.
5. **Monitor and capture learning**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director; Analytics maintains reporting validity.
   * **Action:** Update channel plan, campaign records, and performance feed; track accepted actions; compare results at the next checkpoint; submit reusable learning.
   * **Decision point:** Repeated uncertainty invokes MKT-005; cross-functional conflict invokes COS-006.
   * **Evidence or record:** Updated scorecard, action status, lesson.
   * **Handoff:** Monthly/quarterly executive reviews as material.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Continue/adjust/reduce/pause channel | Fit, qualified reach, efficiency, risk, opportunity cost | Marketing Director | None within approved constraints | Update channel plan |
| Change KPI definition/method | Measurement evidence | Director of Analytics | None within measurement authority | KPI governance path |
| Change offer/funnel or creative | Domain evidence | VP Revenue / Creative Director | Applicable authority | Functional procedure/handoff |
| Material channel investment or priority change | Decision-ready case | CEO | CEO | COS-001 |

## Decision Rules

Marketing owns channel selection and corrective distribution action, not revenue, creative, community, or measurement decisions. Correlation is not treated as causation; unfavorable results and limitations remain visible. Expansion requires validated fit and capacity, and material investment requires CEO approval.

## Escalation Conditions

| Condition | Escalation Level | Escalation Recipient | Required Information | Execution Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Channel action within constraints | Department | Marketing Director | Scorecard and rationale | Decide/monitor |
| Conflicting functional interpretation or capacity | Cross-functional | Chief of Staff via COS-006 | Evidence, positions, impact | Pause contested commitment |
| Material investment/priority, strategy, significant risk | Executive | CEO via COS-001 | Options and recommendation | No reserved commitment |
| Measurement integrity failure | Department/cross-functional | Director of Analytics; Chief of Staff if impact spans functions | Quality evidence | Qualify or pause decision |

## Success Criteria

* Each review produces a traceable channel decision or evidence plan, not reporting alone.
* Performance feeds Analytics-governed KPIs and preserves attribution limitations.
* Corrective actions retain correct functional ownership and contain no implied revenue or creative authority.
* Material investment and priority changes receive CEO approval.

## Risks and Controls

| Risk | Impact | Control | Control Owner | Required Evidence | Escalation Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Misattribution | Wrong investment | Analytics methodology and alternatives | Director of Analytics | Quality/attribution note | Material uncertainty |
| Vanity-metric optimization | Weak customer value | Qualified outcome hierarchy | Marketing Director | Decision rationale | Reach rises while quality declines materially |
| Unauthorized domain action | Ownership conflict | Bounded handoff/acceptance | Chief of Staff | Action map | Disputed assignment |
| Survivorship/selective reporting | Bad decision | Full scorecard and unfavorable findings | Marketing Director | Source-linked report | Omitted material result |

## Exceptions

| Permitted Exception | Authorizing Role | Conditions and Limits | Required Documentation | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early review on material signal | Marketing Director | Comparison limitations explicit | Trigger, data limits, interim action | Confirm at normal cadence |
| Bounded action with incomplete data | Marketing Director; Analytics validates limits | Reversible; customer-safe; no material investment | Assumptions, guardrails, expiry | MKT-005 or next review |

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may assemble governed scorecards, detect anomalies, label evidence, compare periods, monitor actions, and draft reviews. Humans retain interpretation, causal judgment, channel disposition, targets, functional acceptance, risk judgment, and reserved approvals. Automation may not redefine metrics, hide unfavorable results, change channel spend, alter offers/assets, or assign another function.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Monthly; quarterly portfolio synthesis.
* **Event-based review triggers:** threshold breach, data failure, material channel change, repeated underperformance, governance change, automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** Marketing Director.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Director of Analytics, VP Revenue, Creative Director, Director of Community, Chief of Staff, CEO for reserved changes.
