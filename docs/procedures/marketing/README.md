**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Marketing Director  
**Approver:** CEO  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-19

This directory contains the controlled Marketing Director procedures listed in the [Executive Procedure Catalog](../PROCEDURE-CATALOG.md).

## Procedure Sequence

1. [MKT-001: Campaign Planning](MKT-001-campaign-planning.md) creates an approved, measurable campaign baseline and accepted cross-functional handoffs.
2. [MKT-002: Campaign Launch](MKT-002-campaign-launch.md) verifies readiness, authorizes launch within Marketing authority, activates distribution, and confirms handoffs.
3. [MKT-004: Channel Performance Review](MKT-004-channel-performance-review.md) evaluates channel fit and performance and determines Marketing corrective action.
4. [MKT-005: Marketing Experiment Review](MKT-005-marketing-experiment-review.md) evaluates completed or checkpointed marketing experiments and captures reusable learning.
5. [MKT-006: Partnership Evaluation](MKT-006-partnership-evaluation.md) evaluates marketing, referral, and distribution partnerships and governs the Marketing recommendation or decision path.

Each procedure may be triggered independently. References are handoffs, not prerequisites: MKT-001 governs campaign design and approval; MKT-002 governs readiness and activation; MKT-004 governs channel decisions; MKT-005 governs marketing experiment disposition; and MKT-006 governs partnerships. CEO-reserved decisions use COS-001, approved strategic initiatives use COS-002, cross-functional conflicts use COS-006, and governance conflicts use COS-007.

Marketing owns campaigns, launches, channels, distribution, sequencing, promotional priorities, partnerships, and marketing experiments within approved strategy and resources. VP Revenue owns offers, pricing recommendations, funnels, conversion, and sales; every pricing change requires CEO approval. Creative owns content and creative expression. Analytics owns KPI definitions, methods, measurement quality, and experiment evaluation. Community owns engagement, moderation, and advocacy. The Chief of Staff coordinates cross-functional work and repository governance without assuming Marketing accountability.

All records follow the Organizational Knowledge Standard. Marketing retains substantive ownership of marketing knowledge; the Chief of Staff governs repository placement and lifecycle.
