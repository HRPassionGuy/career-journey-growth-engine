# MKT-002: Campaign Launch

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Accountable Executive:** Marketing Director

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Authorize and activate a campaign only when its approved baseline, assets, channels, measurement, operational handoffs, and safeguards are ready, then establish controlled monitoring and response.

## Trigger

An approved campaign reaches its launch-readiness checkpoint or an authorized relaunch is requested.

## Preconditions

* An approved, versioned campaign baseline identifies authority, scope, owner, timing, channels, and launch criteria.
* Revenue-owned offer/funnel terms and any pricing approval are final; Creative-approved assets are available.
* Analytics has validated measurement and instrumentation criteria.
* Required participants have accepted their bounded launch duties.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Freeze scope and verify authority**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director.
   * **Action:** Confirm baseline version, launch window, channels, audience, budget constraint, owner, approvals, and changes since planning. Reject undocumented scope changes.
   * **Decision point:** Any material change returns to the applicable owner and approval path; reserved change uses COS-001.
   * **Evidence or record:** Launch-control record and approval links.
   * **Handoff:** Frozen package to readiness checks.
2. **Run cross-functional readiness gates**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director coordinates; each functional owner signs its gate.
   * **Action:** Confirm Revenue path and terms; Creative assets and usage; Community staffing where applicable; channel access, schedule, links, consent/privacy, accessibility, claims evidence, rollback, incident contacts, and capacity.
   * **Decision point:** Marketing cannot waive another function's failed gate. Unresolved ownership/capacity conflict invokes COS-006.
   * **Evidence or record:** Gate result, owner, timestamp, deficiency, cure date.
   * **Handoff:** Passing package to measurement verification.
3. **Verify measurement in the live path**
   * **Responsible:** Director of Analytics validates; Marketing supplies access/context.
   * **Action:** Test approved events, links, source tags, segmentation, dashboard/reporting route, baseline, attribution limitations, guardrails, and alerts using non-customer-impacting checks where practical.
   * **Decision point:** A failed primary-measure or material data-quality check blocks launch unless Analytics documents that reliable evaluation remains possible and the applicable authority accepts the bounded risk.
   * **Evidence or record:** Test results and Analytics sign-off.
   * **Handoff:** Measurement-ready package to go/no-go.
4. **Make and record go/no-go decision**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director within authority.
   * **Action:** Decide go, conditional go, hold, or cancel against explicit gates. State conditions, monitoring window, decision contacts, rollback triggers, and first review time.
   * **Decision point:** Marketing owns the launch decision for a campaign already approved within delegated authority. The decision does not override CEO, Revenue, Creative, Analytics, or Community authority.
   * **Evidence or record:** Timestamped go/no-go decision and dissent.
   * **Handoff:** Authorized activation instructions to channel owners.
5. **Activate, confirm, and hand off operations**
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director activates distribution; functional owners perform accepted work.
   * **Action:** Release the approved sequence, verify public/customer paths, confirm delivery and tracking, notify stakeholders, and initiate Revenue conversion monitoring, Creative correction route, Analytics reporting, and Community engagement/voice-of-customer handoffs.
   * **Decision point:** Stop or rollback for incorrect terms, unapproved assets, broken conversion path, material tracking failure, customer harm, privacy/ethical issue, or significant risk.
   * **Evidence or record:** Activation log, live-path checks, notifications, incident record.
   * **Handoff:** MKT-004 receives channel evidence; MKT-005 receives experiment record if applicable.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Go, conditional go, hold, cancel | All gates, approvals, risk, rollback readiness | Marketing Director | None within approved scope | Launch or remediate |
| Accept offer/funnel/creative/measurement/community readiness | Domain standards | Respective functional owner | None within domain | Gate pass/fail |
| Launch with reserved or materially changed scope | Decision package | CEO | CEO | COS-001 then re-baseline |
| Emergency stop of distribution | Actual or credible material harm/control failure | Marketing Director; domain owner may stop affected element | None to contain harm | Stop, record, escalate |

## Decision Rules

Marketing alone authorizes campaign distribution after all required domain gates pass, within the approved baseline. Any participant may stop its own affected work for credible material harm or failed control; Marketing coordinates campaign pause. A conditional go cannot waive missing pricing approval, materially broken conversion path, unapproved creative, unusable primary measurement, or legal/ethical/privacy safeguards.

## Escalation Conditions

| Condition | Escalation Level | Escalation Recipient | Required Information | Execution Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Correctable Marketing readiness gap | Department | Marketing Director | Failed gate and cure | Hold affected activation |
| Cross-functional gate dispute/capacity failure | Cross-functional | Chief of Staff via COS-006 | Owner positions, impact, deadline | Hold launch |
| Reserved change or significant risk | Executive | CEO via COS-001 | Evidence, options, recommendation | No launch/affected work |
| Live customer, trust, privacy, ethical, or pricing error | Executive as materiality requires | Functional owner, Chief of Staff, CEO | Incident facts, containment, impact | Stop/rollback immediately |

## Success Criteria

* Launch occurs only from a current approved baseline with every blocking gate passed.
* Every required cross-functional operational handoff is accepted and initiated.
* Measurement begins at activation and campaign performance feeds Analytics-governed reporting.
* Incidents are contained without Marketing assuming another function's authority.

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may check versions, links, fields, schedules, permissions, tracking tests, acknowledgments, and alerts; distribute only after recorded authorization; and assemble incident evidence. Humans retain go/no-go, domain sign-off, harm judgment, rollback/restart, and reserved decisions. Automation may not infer approval, waive gates, substitute assets/terms, redefine measures, or commit another function.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Quarterly and after each material launch incident.
* **Event-based review triggers:** failed gate, rollback, unauthorized change, missed handoff, tracking failure, repeated exception, governance change, automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** Marketing Director.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Chief of Staff, Director of Analytics, VP Revenue, Creative Director, Director of Community, CEO for reserved changes.
