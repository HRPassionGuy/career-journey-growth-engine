# MKT-001: Campaign Planning

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Accountable Executive:** Marketing Director

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Create an executable, customer-value-led campaign plan that defines audience, problem, positioning, channels, timing, desired action, dependencies, risks, and measurement while preserving functional authority.

## Trigger

An approved objective, marketing calendar cycle, qualified campaign opportunity, launch recommendation, performance gap, or CEO request requires campaign planning.

## Preconditions

* The objective and customer problem are defined and within approved strategy.
* A single Marketing accountable owner and available campaign capacity are confirmed.
* Any offer, funnel, conversion, pricing, or creative premise has been provided or validated by its functional owner.
* No reserved commitment is represented as approved.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Register and qualify the campaign**
   * **Objective:** Establish one traceable campaign candidate.
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director.
   * **Required inputs:** Trigger, objective, audience evidence.
   * **Action:** Assign an ID; search active plans and the repository; state audience, problem, desired action, horizon, facts, assumptions, expected value, confidence, and proposed owner.
   * **Decision point:** Merge duplicates; return non-Marketing work to its owner; route strategic initiatives to COS-002 only after applicable approval.
   * **Evidence or record:** Candidate record and duplicate search.
   * **Handoff:** Qualified candidate to design.
2. **Define campaign strategy**
   * **Objective:** Select a coherent, educational distribution approach.
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director.
   * **Required inputs:** Audience insight, objective, current calendar, channel evidence.
   * **Action:** Define positioning, customer problem, value, desired action, channel roles, cadence, sequence, timeline, segmentation, organic/paid status, referral or partnership use, and stop conditions. Choose channels for audience fit and durable value, not popularity.
   * **Decision point:** Marketing approves channels, timing, cadence, distribution, sequencing, and promotional priority within approved strategy/resources. Paid commitments that constitute material investment require CEO approval.
   * **Evidence or record:** Strategy rationale, alternatives, opportunity cost, confidence.
   * **Handoff:** Requirements to functional owners.
3. **Obtain bounded functional inputs and acceptances**
   * **Objective:** Protect ownership while making the campaign executable.
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director coordinates; each executive owns domain output.
   * **Required inputs:** Draft strategy and dependency requests.
   * **Action:** Request Revenue-owned offer/funnel inputs; issue Creative an asset brief without prescribing creative expression; request Community participation where applicable; document deliverables, acceptance criteria, dates, constraints, and owners.
   * **Decision point:** Unaccepted dependencies or ownership disputes invoke COS-006. Marketing may adjust distribution requirements but may not create assets, design offers, set pricing, or assign community operations.
   * **Evidence or record:** Dated acceptance and responsibility map.
   * **Handoff:** Accepted plan to measurement design.
4. **Validate measurement, risk, and readiness**
   * **Objective:** Make outcomes objectively measurable before launch.
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director; Director of Analytics validates measurement.
   * **Required inputs:** Baseline, KPI proposal, instrumentation, risk list.
   * **Action:** Define primary/secondary indicators, baseline, target set by Marketing or business owner, method, sources, attribution limits, reporting cadence, experiment flag, guardrails, and data-quality checks. Assess customer trust, claims, privacy, capacity, channel, dependency, brand, and revenue risks.
   * **Decision point:** Material measurement gaps block launch readiness; significant risk or reserved authority goes to CEO via COS-001.
   * **Evidence or record:** Analytics validation and risk/control record.
   * **Handoff:** Validated brief to approval.
5. **Approve and baseline the campaign**
   * **Objective:** Produce an unambiguous authorization and launch package.
   * **Responsible:** Marketing Director within authority; CEO for reserved matters.
   * **Required inputs:** Complete brief, acceptances, measurement validation, approval requirements.
   * **Action:** Approve, conditionally approve, defer, reject, or escalate. Record scope, dates, budget constraint, owners, assets, channels, measures, guardrails, required approvals, change control, and launch/no-launch criteria.
   * **Decision point:** Marketing campaign approval is final only within approved organizational strategy, priorities, resources, and non-reserved authority. CEO approval is mandatory for reserved elements; Revenue approval is required for revenue-owned substance but does not replace Marketing campaign approval.
   * **Evidence or record:** Signed campaign baseline and all approval references.
   * **Handoff:** MKT-002 receives launch package; Chief of Staff receives portfolio handoff if strategic.

## Decision Points

| Decision | Criteria and Evidence | Decision Owner | Required Approver | Resulting Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Approve campaign plan | Complete brief, capacity, accepted dependencies, measurement, risk | Marketing Director | None within delegated authority | Baseline and MKT-002 |
| Approve Revenue or Creative substance | Domain evidence and standards | VP Revenue / Creative Director | Applicable authority | Accepted input or revision |
| Approve pricing, strategy, major investment, major priority, significant risk | Decision-ready package | CEO | CEO | COS-001 decision |
| Validate KPI definition/method | Data and measurement plan | Director of Analytics | None within measurement authority | Validate or block measurement readiness |

## Decision Rules

Marketing is accountable for campaign planning and may approve the campaign within delegated authority. This approval never authorizes pricing, offer, funnel, sales, creative, community, analytics, automation, strategic direction, or material investment decisions. One primary campaign may proceed only within confirmed capacity; additional primary campaigns require documented capacity and priority resolution.

## Escalation Conditions

| Condition | Escalation Level | Escalation Recipient | Required Information | Execution Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Pricing, strategy, material investment, organizational priority, significant/mission risk | Executive | CEO via COS-001 | Evidence, alternatives, recommendation | No affected commitment |
| Governance conflict | Executive | COS-007; CEO if unresolved | Sources and compliant options | Stop conflicting path |

## Success Criteria

* Every campaign has one Marketing owner, explicit approval authority, accepted dependencies, measures, controls, completion criteria, and launch gates.
* No revenue, creative, community, analytics, or automation authority is assigned to Marketing.
* Every significant campaign produces a complete brief and Analytics-ready KPI handoff.
* No campaign starts before all blocking approvals and owner acceptances are recorded.

## Future Automation Considerations

AI may deduplicate candidates, assemble evidence, validate required fields, draft schedules, monitor acceptances, and route approved handoffs. Humans retain campaign approval, domain validation, target ownership, risk judgment, resource commitment, and reserved decisions. Automation may not create strategy, approve campaigns, redefine KPIs, create creative assets, alter offers/pricing, commit another function, or bypass a gate.

## Review Cadence

* **Scheduled review:** Quarterly; campaign calendar monthly.
* **Event-based review triggers:** approval error, launch failure, repeated rework, ownership conflict, KPI gap, governance change, or automation proposal.
* **Review owner:** Marketing Director.
* **Supporting reviewers:** Chief of Staff, Director of Analytics, VP Revenue, Creative Director, Director of Community, and CEO for reserved changes.
