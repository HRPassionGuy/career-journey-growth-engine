# ANA-005: Measurement Validation

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Accountable Executive:** Director of Analytics

Governing authority: [Project Constitution](../../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).

---

## Purpose

Confirm that a measure, data source, calculation, report, or analytical claim is sufficiently accurate, consistent, traceable, and fit for its stated decision use.

## Scope

This procedure governs recurring Analytics work for measurement validation, from intake through decision, handoff, and knowledge capture. It does not authorize another function's work or any CEO-reserved decision.

## Trigger

A new or changed measurement is proposed; a material discrepancy or quality concern appears; or scheduled validation is due.

## Preconditions

* The request, objective, accountable owner, intended decision, planning horizon, and required date are documented.
* Applicable approvals, source records, access, capacity, and current controlled definitions are available.
* Material gaps, conflicts, confidentiality constraints, and known risks are disclosed before execution.

|---|---|---|
| Decision question, objective, scope, target or acceptance criteria | Requesting/accountable executive | Current, specific, mission-aligned, and within authority |
| Domain evidence and prior decisions | Director of Analytics / functional source owners | Traceable, dated, relevant, and limitations disclosed |
| KPI definitions, methods, source and quality record | Director of Analytics | Governed and validated for intended use |
| Dependencies, risks, capacity and required approvals | Affected executives / Chief of Staff / CEO | Owner-confirmed; reserved approvals explicit |

## Procedure Steps

1. **Classify the validation request**
   * **Responsible:** Director of Analytics; supporting owners contribute only within charter.
   * **Action:** Record the measure or claim, intended use, materiality, owner, consumers, current definition, source systems, transformations, comparison periods, and validation acceptance criteria.
   * **Decision point:** Continue only when required evidence and authority are sufficient; otherwise return for correction, qualify the result, or escalate at the appropriate level.
   * **Evidence or record:** Dated inputs, analysis, owner confirmations, decision rationale, exceptions, and version.
   * **Handoff:** Accepted output to next procedure step.
2. **Trace definition, source, and calculation**
   * **Responsible:** Director of Analytics; supporting owners contribute only within charter.
   * **Action:** Trace lineage from source event through extraction, transformation, calculation, storage, and presentation; compare implementation to the KPI registry and identify undocumented overrides.
   * **Decision point:** Continue only when required evidence and authority are sufficient; otherwise return for correction, qualify the result, or escalate at the appropriate level.
   * **Evidence or record:** Dated inputs, analysis, owner confirmations, decision rationale, exceptions, and version.
   * **Handoff:** Accepted output to next procedure step.
3. **Test quality and reconciliation**
   * **Responsible:** Director of Analytics; supporting owners contribute only within charter.
   * **Action:** Test completeness, validity, uniqueness, consistency, timeliness, reconciliation, edge cases, access, reproducibility, historical comparability, and materiality of every defect.
   * **Decision point:** Continue only when required evidence and authority are sufficient; otherwise return for correction, qualify the result, or escalate at the appropriate level.
   * **Evidence or record:** Dated inputs, analysis, owner confirmations, decision rationale, exceptions, and version.
   * **Handoff:** Accepted output to next procedure step.
4. **Record validation disposition**
   * **Responsible:** Director of Analytics; supporting owners contribute only within charter.
   * **Action:** Classify validated, validated with limitation, or rejected; assign correction owners, prevent unsupported publication, record affected reports and restatement needs, and schedule revalidation.
   * **Decision point:** Continue only when required evidence and authority are sufficient; otherwise return for correction, qualify the result, or escalate at the appropriate level.
   * **Evidence or record:** Dated inputs, analysis, owner confirmations, decision rationale, exceptions, and version.
   * **Handoff:** Accepted output to named recipients and the controlled repository.

## Decision Rules

Director of Analytics owns Analytics judgment and this procedure's disposition. Analytics alone owns KPI definitions, measurement methods, data quality, and reporting consistency; business executives own objectives, targets, interpretation, corrective action, and outcomes. The Chief of Staff coordinates cross-functional governance without assuming functional ownership. No procedure result authorizes pricing, strategy, major investment, organizational priorities, significant risk acceptance, mission changes, or another executive's work.

## Success Criteria

* The procedure produces a timely, evidence-bounded, traceable disposition with one accountable executive.
* Measurement content is Analytics-governed and functional actions remain with their chartered owners.
* Risks, uncertainty, exceptions, approvals, handoffs, and completion conditions are explicit.
* Required decisions and learning are accepted into the appropriate controlled records without duplicate authority.

## Future Automation Considerations

Automation may validate required fields, retrieve approved sources, calculate approved methods, flag thresholds or conflicts, assemble drafts, route handoffs, and monitor review dates. Humans retain domain judgment, evidence interpretation, exception authorization, owner acceptance, risk decisions, and all CEO-reserved approvals. Automation may not create authority, change business logic, redefine KPIs, conceal uncertainty, approve its source procedure, or execute a contested or reserved decision.
