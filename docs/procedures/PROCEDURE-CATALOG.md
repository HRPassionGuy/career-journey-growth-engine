# Executive Procedure Catalog

## Purpose

This catalog is the authoritative register of all Executive Procedures for the Career Journey Growth Engine.

Every Executive Procedure must appear in this catalog.

---

## Relationship to the Architecture

This catalog is governed by:

* The [Project Constitution](../PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md).
* [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).
* [Executive Charters](../../agents/).
* [Executive Operating Standards](../standards/).
* The [Executive Procedure Standard](../standards/executive-procedure-standard.md).
* [Architecture Decision Records](../adr/).

This catalog does not define governance. It indexes approved and planned procedures.

---

## Procedure ID Convention

| Executive | Prefix |
|-----------|--------|
| Chief of Staff | COS |
| VP Revenue | REV |
| Marketing Director | MKT |
| Director of Community | COM |
| Creative Director | CRE |
| Director of Analytics | ANA |
| Director of Research & Product Development | RPD |
| Director of Automation | AUT |

Procedure IDs follow this format:

`PREFIX-001`

Examples:

* `COS-001`
* `REV-001`
* `ANA-004`

---

## Procedure Status

Use only these planning statuses within the catalog:

* Planned
* In Development
* Active
* Retired

These catalog statuses track portfolio planning only. They do not replace or modify the lifecycle defined in the Executive Procedure Standard.

## Catalog Governance

The catalog is maintained by the Chief of Staff.

Adding or retiring procedures requires executive approval.

Changing procedure content must occur within the procedure document itself.

The catalog exists only to govern the Executive Procedure portfolio.
