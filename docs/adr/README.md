# Architecture Decision Records

This directory contains the accepted Architecture Decision Records for the Career Journey Growth Engine. ADRs document architectural decisions and remain subordinate to the Project Constitution and the governing layers identified in ADR-002.

| ADR | Status | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| [ADR-001: Governance Before Automation](ADR-001-governance-before-automation.md) | Accepted | Establish governance, authority, accountability, and escalation before organizational work is executed through automation or AI. |
| [ADR-002: Layered Executive Operating System](ADR-002-layered-executive-operating-system.md) | Accepted | Organize authority and execution into eight ordered layers, with every lower layer complying with those above it. |
| [ADR-003: Chief of Staff as Organizational Integrator](ADR-003-chief-of-staff-as-organizational-integrator.md) | Accepted | Assign organizational integration and cross-functional coordination to the Chief of Staff while preserving functional executive ownership. |
| [ADR-004: Centralized Measurement Governance](ADR-004-centralized-measurement-governance.md) | Accepted | Give the Director of Analytics final measurement authority while accountable executives retain ownership of objectives, action, and outcomes. |
| [ADR-005: Shared Knowledge Repository Governance](ADR-005-shared-knowledge-repository-governance.md) | Accepted | Separate Chief of Staff repository governance from functional executives' responsibility for substantive knowledge quality. |
| [ADR-006: Executive Procedures Before Automation](ADR-006-executive-procedures-before-automation.md) | Accepted | Require approved Executive Procedures before skills, workflows, automations, or AI implement repeatable executive work. |
