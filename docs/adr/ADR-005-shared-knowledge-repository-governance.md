# ADR-005: Shared Knowledge Repository Governance

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-19
- Decision Owners: Chief of Staff

## Context

Institutional knowledge must be trustworthy, discoverable, current, and reusable across the organization. Central repository administration is necessary, but it must not transfer responsibility for specialized knowledge away from the executive who produces it.

## Decision

The Chief of Staff governs the structure, lifecycle, acceptance, publication, accessibility, and archival of the shared organizational Knowledge Repository. Functional executives remain responsible for the accuracy, completeness, validation, review, and maintenance of knowledge produced in their areas.

## Rationale

This division provides consistent repository governance while preserving substantive accountability with the relevant functional expert. It supports organizational learning without making the Chief of Staff responsible for validating specialized content.

## Consequences

Knowledge assets have a clear handoff: the functional executive validates and submits the asset; the Chief of Staff accepts, places, publishes, and governs its repository lifecycle. Acceptance does not transfer responsibility for substantive accuracy. Functional executives maintain their assets, while the Chief of Staff coordinates repository-level review and archival.
