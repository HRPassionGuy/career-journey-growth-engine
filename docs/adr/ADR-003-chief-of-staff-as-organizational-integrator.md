# ADR-003: Chief of Staff as Organizational Integrator

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-19
- Decision Owners: Chief of Staff

## Context

Cross-functional work requires coordination, shared governance, portfolio visibility, and an executive operating cadence without creating duplicate ownership or weakening functional accountability.

## Decision

The Chief of Staff owns cross-functional coordination, governance integration, executive operating cadence, the executive decision register, the organizational initiative portfolio, and shared repository governance. Functional executives retain ownership of their own departments.

## Rationale

A single organizational integrator reduces conflicting priorities, unclear ownership, duplication, and communication gaps. Keeping functional authority with the chartered executives preserves expertise and the constitutional requirement for one accountable owner.

## Consequences

Cross-functional initiatives receive consistent coordination, portfolio tracking, and escalation. The Chief of Staff may request inputs, consolidate recommendations, and confirm governance records, but may not become the default owner, perform specialized functional work, or override an executive within that executive's domain. Material strategic decisions remain with the CEO.
