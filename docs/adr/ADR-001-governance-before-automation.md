# ADR-001: Governance Before Automation

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-19
- Decision Owners: Chief of Staff; Director of Automation

## Context

Organizational work may be executed through prompts, workflows, agents, and automations. Without prior governance, those mechanisms could obscure accountability, exceed delegated authority, or reproduce an undefined process at scale.

## Decision

Governance, authority, accountability, and escalation rules must be established before prompts, workflows, agents, or automations are allowed to execute organizational work.

## Rationale

The Project Constitution requires clear ownership, evidence-based decisions, and escalation beyond an executive's authority. Establishing those controls first ensures that automation supports approved organizational behavior and preserves human executive accountability.

## Consequences

Automation becomes more reliable, auditable, and aligned with organizational priorities. Work may take longer to automate because ownership, boundaries, escalation, and acceptance criteria must be explicit first. Undefined or disputed processes cannot proceed directly to automated execution.
