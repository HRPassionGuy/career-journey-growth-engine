# ADR-004: Centralized Measurement Governance

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-19
- Decision Owners: Director of Analytics

## Context

Executives need consistent KPIs and trustworthy reporting while retaining accountability for the business results within their functions. Dispersed authority over metric definitions and methods would make comparisons unreliable and weaken organizational learning.

## Decision

The Director of Analytics has final authority over KPI definitions, calculation methodology, measurement quality, data standards, and reporting consistency. Accountable executives own business objectives, targets, interpretation, corrective action, and performance outcomes.

## Rationale

Central measurement governance creates a common evidence base and protects data integrity. Separating measurement authority from performance ownership prevents inconsistent definitions without transferring functional decisions or outcomes to Analytics.

## Consequences

KPIs, dashboards, and reports must follow Analytics standards, and material KPI changes require documentation and applicable approval. Functional executives collaborate with Analytics on measures but remain accountable for targets, decisions, corrective action, and results. Analytics may challenge measurement quality but may not override strategic or functional decisions.
